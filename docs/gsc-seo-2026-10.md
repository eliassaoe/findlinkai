# Search Console read-out, October 2026: what changed and why

**Date:** 2026-10-17 · **Source:** Google Search Console (`sc-domain:linkfinderai.com`) via
Searchata, 90 days to 2026-10-16 unless stated. Read `docs/organic-to-paid-2026-10.md`
first: it says which pages actually sign people up. This doc is about getting more of the
clicks those pages already rank for.

## 1. A large share of impressions are bots, so filter before judging CTR

Nine pages carry ~367k of ~887k impressions (41%) and ~450 clicks (2.5%):
`/company-employee-finder`, `/find-company-employee-count`, `/linkedin-company-employees-api`,
`/best-lead-generation-api`, `/extract-name-from-linkedin-url`, `/linkedin-company-scraper-api`,
`/scrape-linkedIn-jobs`, `/company-employee-count-api`, `/extract-job-title-from-linkedin-url`.

Their top queries are not typed by people:
- `company team lookup`: 54.8k impressions in 28 days, position 4.3, 0 clicks.
- `cannot assist with linkedin lookup from email`: an AI assistant's refusal text sent to Google as a search.
- `linkedin profile ashutosh d b248877 job title company companyurl`, `number of employees at finderthat.fi`: agent fan-out queries.
- 600-word persona prompts on `/best-lead-generation-api`.

`/linkedin-company-employees-api` gets 44.3k impressions, and only 40 of them come from outside the USA. Desktop is 752k
impressions at 1.3% CTR; mobile is 97k at 8.2%.

**Rule:** don't rewrite a title because these pages show low CTR. People aren't the ones seeing those titles. When
measuring a title change, filter to `country != USA` (or device = mobile) and compare per page.
Clicks from the USA are real (~2.6k a quarter on the phone finder), but the US *impression* count is not
a usable CTR denominator.

## 2. The 2026-09-12 bulk retitle: kept, except on the email finder

`retitle_for_bulk_intent.py` changed the tool pages from free-lookup framing to bulk/CSV/API framing.
Non-US clicks, 28 days after (09-15 → 10-12) vs 28 days before (08-15 → 09-11):

| page | clicks before → after | CTR before → after | position |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| /linkedin-phone-number-finder | 927 → 1,038 | 15.5% → 14.3% | 7.2 → 6.4 |
| **/linkedin-email-finder** | **597 → 268** | **4.5% → 3.4%** | **7.6 → 9.0** |
| /linkedin-search-by-email | 239 → 208 | 4.4% → 9.5% | 10.7 → 9.9 |
| /linkedin-url-finder | 159 → 117 | 3.8% → 4.5% | 5.5 → 5.6 |
| /linkedin-profile-scraper | 76 → 75 | 4.7% → 7.0% | 13.1 → 7.8 |
| /company-url-finder | 59 → 64 | 4.3% → 5.4% | 4.4 → 3.4 |

CTR went **up** everywhere except the email finder. Its head query (`linkedin email finder`, all
countries) fell from 232 to 104 clicks a month (CTR 4.1% → 3.0%, position 7.0 → 8.2). `free linkedin email finder`
fell from 8 to 1 click. That page's pool is only 2.05% bulk-intent (see the script's docstring), so it pays
most for removing "Free".

**Decision (owner, 2026-10-17):** hybrid title on `/linkedin-email-finder` only:
`LinkedIn Email Finder - Free Lookup, Bulk CSV or API`. It brings back the free single lookup and keeps the
bulk qualifier visible. Leave the other retitled pages alone. Re-running `retitle_for_bulk_intent.py`
reports this page as "title changed since - skipped", so it won't undo this.

**Re-check on ~2026-11-15:** non-US clicks and CTR for `linkedin email finder` on this page, compared with
08-15 → 09-11. If CTR is back near 4% and bulk touches from this page haven't dropped, keep it.

## 3. Instagram: the consolidation worked, and the July drop is a separate problem

`/instagram-profile-scraper` became a stub (meta refresh + canonical + noindex) on 2026-05-22. Within
days its clicks moved onto `/instagram-profile-url-finder`, which went from ~10 to ~100 clicks a day. **Do not
undo the consolidation.**

The loss started around **2026-07-09**. `/instagram-profile-url-finder` slid from position ~7.5 to 12–16
through August, and clicks fell from ~100 a day to ~20. Its title, H1 and canonical didn't change in July (those commits
only touched the related-links block), so this isn't self-inflicted. Position is back to ~9 in October
(~25 clicks a day). The fix is ranking (content and links), not the snippet. The title was also 76 characters and
truncated, and didn't contain the head query `instagram profile finder` (2.9k impressions, pos 9.3). It now reads
`Instagram Profile Finder - Find Anyone's Instagram URL`.

## 5. Not done, and why

- **Other "one per lookup" claims.** `company-details-finder`, `company-description-finder`,
  `company-founded-year`, `company-industry-finder`, `email-to-phone-number-finder` and `app-integrations`
  use the same sentence. These don't map cleanly to a `creditCosts` key, so check which enrichment
  each page runs before correcting them. `company-url-finder`, `company-phone-finder`,
  `find-company-employee-count` and `linkedin-url-finder` really are 1 credit.
- **Breadcrumb "Unnamed item"** in URL Inspection is the API's summary label. The BreadcrumbList JSON-LD on these pages has
  names, so there's nothing to fix.
- **`/best-recruitment-sofware-tools`** (typo) is still the URL Google chose as canonical, even though the stub refreshes to the correct
  spelling. GitHub Pages can't send a 301, so it would take Cloudflare or a redirect host. Low value: it's an
  off-topic listicle at pos ~68.
- **`/linkfinderai.com/instagram-profile-url-finder`** got 19–21 impressions in September. No link in this repo produces it, so
  the source is probably external or a past version.
- **Off-topic listicles** (proxies, price scraping, marketing automation, recruitment software, lead scoring) rank at
  pos 30–80 and get almost no clicks. Candidates for noindex. That's an owner decision, not made here.
- **`tests/sales-led-motion.test.mjs`** fails on `main` before and after this change. It's unrelated.
