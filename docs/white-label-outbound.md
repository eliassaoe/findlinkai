## Why they are not there — and why that does not kill white-label

A working agency has a *monthly* contact-data need and is already paying someone
for it — Apollo, Clay, Dropcontact, a VA. That agency does not sign up for a free
tool and run 4,000 lookups in one afternoon. **The behaviour that fills your
database is the behaviour of someone doing a one-off job**, which is exactly what
`docs/data-provider-angle.md` called Shape A: had a list, resolved the list,
left.

So the absence of resellers in the base is not evidence that reselling fails. It
is evidence that **resellers were never going to arrive through a self-serve
signup form.** They have to be gone and got.

Which means the white-label partner is an **outbound** target, not an email-list
target — and that is the good news, because you have nine warm mailboxes and
nothing worth sending from them.

## Reselling is a better cold-outbound angle than Done For You

Comparing the two things the mailboxes could carry:

| | Done For You | White-label reselling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What you must deliver | **booked meetings** — 0 replies from 44 sends so far | **the enrichment API — already works** |
| The ask | buy a service | **take a margin line** |
| How it reads in an inbox | another agency selling to an agency | nobody cold-emails to offer you revenue |
| Deal shape | $750/mo, you deliver | recurring, scales with **their** client count |
| Failure cost | you burn a client they vouched for | a credit refund |

Every agency inbox is full of people selling them tools. **Almost nobody writes
to offer them something to sell.** That asymmetry is the whole reason this angle
should out-reply a DFY pitch, and it asks for a reply rather than a click —
which matters, because per `docs/revenue-levers-2026-08.md` scanners eat clicks
and PostHog flags 100% of email engagement as bots.

**Recommendation: make reselling the first sales campaign the new mailboxes
carry, ahead of Done For You.** It sells the product that works.

## One thing not to do

Do not open a cold email with *"I saw your agency uses LinkFinder AI"* to someone
who does not. It is false, it collapses the moment anyone replies "we don't", and
it makes the real version — sent to the eight who genuinely do — worthless when
it arrives.

The true premise is stronger anyway. To the eight you have their actual numbers.
To everyone else you have something equally true: **they are buying contact data
from someone right now.**

## The two emails

House style per `docs/dfy-activation-campaign.md`: an observation, the thing
named plainly, the mechanics in one sentence, an easy question. No links, no
formatting, reply-not-click.

### A — the eight who really do use it (send today, personal inbox)

> **Subject:** you ran 6,043 lookups in two days
>
> 6,043 lookups on LinkFinder in two days. Nobody does that for themselves —
> that is client work.
>
> So, a question rather than a pitch: do you want to resell it?
>
> Our engine, your brand, wholesale rate. Your client never sees our name and
> never sees our pricing. You set theirs, you keep the spread.
>
> There is a monthly minimum, so it only works if you are already billing clients
> for this — which at 6,043 lookups, you are.
>
> 15 minutes this week?
>
> Eliasse

Swap the number per recipient. `cambium.ai` gets a different opener, because
their number is not volume, it is consistency:

> **Subject:** 22 days
>
> You have come back to LinkFinder on 22 separate days, almost all of it company
> expansion. Out of 7,300 accounts, that is the most consistent usage on the
> platform — which tells me this is a workflow, not an experiment.

### B — cold, to agencies who have never heard of you (after warmup, ~3 weeks)

> **Subject:** reselling it instead of renting it
>
> You run outbound for clients, so you are buying contact data every month —
> Apollo, Clay, someone.
>
> Right now that is a cost line. It could be a margin line.
>
> We are LinkFinder AI — LinkedIn profile to verified email or direct dial, plus
> company data. A handful of agencies run it under their own brand at wholesale.
> Their client never sees us. They set the price and keep the spread.
>
> Not asking you to switch anything you use. Asking whether you would rather sell
> this than pay for it.
>
> Worth 15 minutes?
>
> Eliasse

Subject alternates worth testing: *"a margin line instead of a cost line"* ·
*"your data bill, from the other side"*.

## Sequence

1. **Today** — eight personal emails, real numbers, from the personal inbox. No
   deliverability risk, no warmup needed.
2. **Now** — build the cold list: agencies doing outbound for clients. You sell
   the tool that builds this list; use it. A partner list sourced with your own
   product is also the demo.
3. **~3 weeks** — mailboxes mature (created 29 Aug, 6.8% bounce today — verify
   the list first, get it under 2%). Campaign B goes out as the first sales
   campaign the workspace has ever run.
4. **Only then** Done For You, once there is a meeting booked to point at.

## Expectation

Eight warm emails → 2–3 replies → 1–2 calls → perhaps one partner.
Cold at maturity: 900/month → 1–3% reply on a differentiated ask → 9–27 replies
→ a handful of calls a month.

One agency reselling to 20 clients beats everything the referral program has
produced in its life (12 referrers, $0.28 paid out). It is one or two
relationships, worked by hand. That is what this stage looks like.
