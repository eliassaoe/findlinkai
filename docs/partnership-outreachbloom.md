## The two questions she asked, and the true answers

**1. Which domain is live? → `linkfinderai.com`.**
`CNAME` is `linkfinderai.com`, every canonical and every internal link in the
repo points there, and the 215 URLs in `sitemap.xml` are all on it.
`linkfinderaiapp.com` is an outreach sending domain — outbound runs on
separate domains on purpose so the main domain never carries cold email
(see `OUTBOUND-CRM-AUDIT.md`). Nothing is hosted on it. Every link we ask
for points at `linkfinderai.com`.

**2. Where would OutreachBloom go? → nowhere today.**
Every roundup we publish is tools, APIs or alternatives —
`best-lead-generation-tools.html`, `best-b2b-data-providers.html`, ~215 pages,
and not one of them is an agency list. So the reply commits to building one.

## The commitment made in the reply

Build **`/best-b2b-lead-generation-agencies`** — a real agency roundup in the

## Flag before sending

`done-for-you-outbound.html` sells our own done-for-you outbound service.
OutreachBloom is a B2B lead generation and cold email agency — so we would be
linking to a competitor of that offer from our own site. It is probably still
worth it (their link is the point, and the agency page brings its own traffic),
but it is a real trade and Eliasse should make it knowingly.
