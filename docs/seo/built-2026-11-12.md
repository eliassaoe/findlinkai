## Decisions taken here

- **No new competitor, comparison or `best-*` pages** (`lusha-vs-apollo` and the
  like). `docs/organic-to-paid-2026-10.md`: 36 alternative pages brought ~40
  visitors in 90 days. Waiting for the owner.
- **"Scraper" pages say what the product does.** LinkFinder AI does not log in
  to LinkedIn as anyone. Those pages target the query and send people to
  enrichment, the app's "Search LinkedIn" input, post reactions, or the employee
  finder. No page promises follower scraping, email verification, or a
  YouTube/Facebook/X email lookup.
- **Lead search is described as it is reachable today** (`docs/lead-search.md`):
  the app's bulk card, "No LinkedIn URLs? Search LinkedIn", job title or keywords
  + location, 25-200 people, 1 credit per profile found, then emails at 10. No
  seniority or company-size filter is promised. If the standalone Lead Search is
  re-linked, update `LEAD_SEARCH` in `pages_c_leads.py` and re-run.
- **Pages softened by `soften_tool_pages.py` were not touched**
  (`/linkedin-scraping-api`, `/linkedin-profile-data-extraction`,
  `/linkedin-company-scraper`, `/linkedIn-post-scraper`). They rank on page 5-6
  for "scraper" queries; putting the word back is the owner's call.
- No title, meta description or H1 of an existing page was changed.
- Fixed in passing: a nested comment on `/linkedin-email-finder` printed
  "opening tag. Trimmed to the same…" above the footer.

## French, German and Spanish versions (12 Nov 2026)

`/fr/`, `/de/` and `/es/` versions of the five pages that bring the organic
traffic: `linkedin-phone-number-finder`, `linkedin-email-finder`,
`linkedin-search-by-email`, `linkedin-url-finder`, `company-url-finder`.
Built by `seo_2026_11/i18n/localize.py` from the live English page plus the
dictionaries and native content in `seo_2026_11/i18n/{fr,de,es}.py`.
**Re-run it after any change to one of these English pages**, or the localized
tool drifts from the English one.

- The working tool (form, inline scripts, IDs) is copied from the English page;
  every visible string and the scripts' UI messages are translated. The build
  fails if a kept string has no translation.
- English marketing sections under the tool are not carried over; each version
  gets its own sections and FAQ written for its market's query. Testimonials are
  never republished. Legacy claims that our data contradicts ("95% accuracy",
  "5,000+ sales teams", "60,000 credits a month" for $49) were not translated as
  such.
- Each version: `<html lang>`, self canonical, `og:locale`, a reciprocal
  hreflang set (en, fr, de, es, x-default = English, per Google's localized
  versions guide), FAQPage JSON-LD in the page language, language switcher, no
  automatic redirect by language.
- The English pages only got the hreflang links in `<head>` and the language
  switcher above the footer (`LF-HREFLANG`, `LF-LANGSWITCH` markers).
- `js/lf-bf-banner.js` skips `/fr/ /de/ /es/` (the deal page is English only);
  `js/lf-highticket-cta.js` is not loaded there.
- Still English on those pages: anything injected by shared scripts after a
  lookup (`lf-gate.js` sign-up gate), and the app and sign-up pages themselves.
- Keyword evidence: Semrush FR/DE/ES had no measurable volume for
  LinkedIn-specific phrasings; titles pair the natural local query with the
  English tool name people also type (FR "email finder" 1,300/mo). Notes at the
  top of each language file.
- Re-check ~2026-02-01: impressions and clicks per `/fr/ /de/ /es/` page in
  Search Console (country filter), and that the English pages kept their clicks.
