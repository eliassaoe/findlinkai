2 Crustdata Best for real-time, webhook-driven company & people signals
Starting price: Permanent free tier; paid tiers custom-quoted Free tier: Yes, ongoing (not time-limited)

If ScrapIn's appeal was genuinely live, uncached data, Crustdata is built around the same principle — real-time crawling plus webhook alerts for changes — and matches ScrapIn's target audience of engineering teams building on top of the data layer.

3 Reverse Contact Best for turning an email address into a full profile
Starting price: ~$39/mo Free tier: Check their site

Reverse Contact is narrowly built around one workflow — you already have an email address and need the person's name, title, company, and LinkedIn profile behind it — using OSINT methods rather than a stored database, with API, CSV/XLSX bulk upload, and Zapier/Make integrations.

Tradeoff: it's a point solution for reverse email lookup specifically — if your actual need is finding emails or verifying them rather than reversing a known email, broader tools do more for less.

4 Apollo.io Best for a broader database plus outreach sequencing
Starting price: Free, then ~$49–$119/user/mo (annual) Free tier: Yes, with limited credits

Apollo pairs a large contact database (275M+ contacts) with built-in email sequencing and CRM integration, a different model from ScrapIn's live-scrape-on-demand approach but a fit for teams that want data and outreach in one place rather than a raw API.

Tradeoff: pricing is per-seat, and it won't replicate ScrapIn's real-time, uncached scraping for teams that specifically need freshness guarantees at the individual-request level.

5 Prospeo Best for verified email accuracy at very low cost
Starting price: ~$0.01/email Free tier: 75 free emails/month

If your ScrapIn usage mostly resolved to "get me a verified work email," Prospeo does that one job at a claimed 98% accuracy with transparent per-credit pricing and no pricing-tier cliff to plan around.

Tradeoff: it doesn't attempt ScrapIn's broader real-time profile and company scraping — it's a point solution for email specifically.

Where ScrapIn still wins

ScrapIn's core strength is genuine: real-time, cookie-less LinkedIn and company profile extraction with no cached database behind it, and a 4.4/5 G2 rating from reviewers who specifically praise data freshness and speed over cached alternatives. If your workflow depends on guaranteed live data at the individual-request level and you're comfortable with its pricing structure, it remains a legitimate, capable choice.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best ScrapIn alternative?

It depends on your use case. LinkFinder AI suits teams that want flat, predictable pricing for LinkedIn and company data without a pricing cliff. Crustdata suits engineering teams that specifically want real-time, webhook-driven signals. Reverse Contact suits teams whose main workflow is turning an email address into a profile. Apollo.io suits teams that want a broader database plus outreach. Prospeo suits teams whose main need is verified email accuracy at low cost.

Why do people look for a ScrapIn alternative?

The most common reason is ScrapIn's pricing cliff: a low-cost trial (around $30 for seven days) followed by a steep jump to production pricing, commonly cited in the $500-$2,500+/month range depending on tier. Teams that want to stay on ScrapIn's best rates long-term also need to commit to a 12-month annual contract, though ScrapIn's pay-as-you-go option carries no commitment at all.

Does ScrapIn require an annual contract?

Only on its Annual/Production tier, which ScrapIn's own pricing page describes as a 12-month commitment with custom pricing. Its pay-as-you-go option carries no commitment and no subscription renewal, so the contract requirement isn't universal — it applies specifically to the discounted annual plan.

How much does ScrapIn cost in 2026?

Third-party trackers report a range: a roughly $30 seven-day trial, pay-as-you-go pricing commonly cited around $500-$1,000 to get started, and production/annual plans reported around $2,500/month. Figures vary across sources, so confirm current numbers directly with ScrapIn.
