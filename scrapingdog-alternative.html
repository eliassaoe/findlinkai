Bright Data — best for enterprise-scale, compliance-focused scraping

  • Usage-based pricing is harder to predict than a flat monthly rate

Oxylabs — best for enterprise proxy infrastructure

Apify — best for no-code scraping automation

Apify runs pre-built "Actors" for common scraping tasks (Google Maps, Amazon, social platforms) through a marketplace, plus custom Actor development — a different shape than Scrapingdog's raw request-in, HTML-out API.
