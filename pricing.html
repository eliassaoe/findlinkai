Pricing Questions

Credits are priced per lookup, because some data costs far more to find than others. Company-level lookups are the cheapest; a direct mobile number is the most expensive thing on the list.
  • Company website, phone, email, LinkedIn page or employee list, from a company name = 1 credit
  • Someone's LinkedIn URL from their full name = 1 credit
  • Someone's LinkedIn URL from their email = 5 credits
  • Enrich a company from its LinkedIn page = 6 credits
  • Someone's email from their name = 7 credits
  • Enrich a full LinkedIn profile = 10 credits
  • Email from a LinkedIn profile = 10 credits
  • Direct phone from a LinkedIn profile = 25 credits (was 50 — now half price)
You only pay for the specific field you ask for, and a lookup that finds nothing still costs its credit. What a plan gets you therefore depends on which lookups you run: a company-level workflow (find the LinkedIn URL, then the company email) is 2 credits a lead, so 5,000 credits on Starter is around 2,500 leads. A profile-level workflow (full profile enrichment plus their email) is 20 credits a lead, so the same plan is around 250 leads. Most people land somewhere in between.
Credits volume — that's it. Every plan includes identical features: all enrichment tools, full API access, batch CSV processing, business email and phone discovery, and CSV/JSON/Excel exports. The only thing that changes is how many credits you get per month:
  • Starter: 5,000 credits/mo — roughly 2,500 fully enriched leads
  • Professional: 20,000 credits/mo — roughly 10,000 fully enriched leads
  • Enterprise: 50,000 credits/mo — roughly 25,000 fully enriched leads
Pick the plan that matches your monthly lead volume. Upgrade or downgrade any time.
