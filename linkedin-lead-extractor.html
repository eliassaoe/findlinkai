What the lead extractor does

Most LinkedIn lead extractors are scrapers: they open your search in your logged-in browser and copy the results, which puts your account at risk and still gives you no email addresses. LinkFinder AI's Lead Search works the other way round. You give it a job title and a location, it finds matching LinkedIn profiles without touching your account, and you add the work email for each one in the same run.

You giveYou get back, per person
A job title or keywords (for example Head of Sales)LinkedIn profile URL and full name
A location (city or country)Current job title and company
How many people: 25, 50, 100 or 200Location, then a verified work email if you add it

It runs from the bulk enrichment card in the app ("No LinkedIn URLs? Search LinkedIn"), with no LinkedIn account, Sales Navigator seat or cookie involved. 1 credit per profile found, plus 10 credits per work email: about 11 credits per lead with an email.

How to extract a lead list

  1. Sign up and open the bulk enrichment card in the app, with LinkedIn profile as the input.
  2. Click No LinkedIn URLs? Search LinkedIn, type a job title or keywords (for example Head of Sales) and a location.
  3. Pick 25, 50, 100 or 200 people. The profiles found become your list.
  4. Choose work email as the lookup, run it, and download the CSV or send it to HubSpot or Google Sheets.

Already extracted a list somewhere else?

If you have profile URLs from Sales Navigator, an event, a post or another tool, you do not need to search again. Upload them to the bulk LinkedIn email finder for work emails (10 credits each) or the bulk phone finder for direct dials (50 credits each).

Frequently asked questions

Is the LinkedIn lead extractor safe for my account?

Yes. It never logs in to LinkedIn as you and never asks for a cookie, so there is nothing on your account for LinkedIn to restrict.

Do I need Sales Navigator?

No. The search uses its own access to LinkedIn data and filters by job title or keywords and location. For finer filters such as seniority or company size, build the list elsewhere and upload it.

How much does it cost per lead?

1 credit per LinkedIn profile found, plus 10 credits for the verified work email: about 11 credits per lead with an email.
