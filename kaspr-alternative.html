Best Kaspr Alternatives in 2026

Kaspr is a well-liked Chrome extension for revealing phone numbers and emails straight from LinkedIn profiles, with genuinely strong European phone coverage. Here are 5 real alternatives, ranked and compared honestly, starting with the one we make.

LinkFinder AI covers LinkedIn profile enrichment, email finder, company data, and phone lookup through a single credit pool — no separate phone/direct-email/B2B-email allocations to juggle, and no requirement to have any minimum number of LinkedIn connections. 2 Lusha Best for a similar browser-extension workflow with broader coverage
Starting price: Free, then ~$37/user/mo Free tier: Yes, ~70 credits/month

Lusha is the most direct functional competitor to Kaspr — also a browser-extension-first tool focused on phone and email reveals — but its extension works on company websites and other pages too, not just LinkedIn, with a larger overall database.

Tradeoff: phone-reveal credits cost more than email credits, and Lusha's own annual-plan credits reset with no rollover — a different but comparable "read the fine print" issue to Kaspr's credit-type complexity.

3 Apollo.io Best for a database plus outreach sequencing in one platform
Starting price: Free, then ~$49–$119/user/mo (annual) Free tier: Yes, with limited credits

Apollo combines a large contact database (230M+ contacts) with multi-channel sequencing, a built-in dialer, and CRM integration — a real step up from Kaspr's LinkedIn-only lookup model for teams that want data and outreach execution together.

Tradeoff: pricing is per-seat, and it's a bigger, more complex platform to learn than Kaspr's single-purpose Chrome extension.

4 Cognism Best for global phone-verified data at enterprise scale
Starting price: Custom, commonly ~$15,000–$25,000/year Free tier: None publicly listed

Worth knowing: Cognism acquired Kaspr in April 2022 and positions it as the lighter, cheaper entry point into its own data ecosystem. If you outgrow Kaspr's LinkedIn-only coverage, Cognism itself is the natural upgrade path, with global phone-verified data and intent signals.

Tradeoff: it's sold like enterprise software — custom quotes, annual contracts, no self-serve signup — a much bigger commitment than Kaspr's per-seat plans.

5 Cleanlist Best for multi-source enrichment beyond LinkedIn, at a flat rate
Starting price: ~$79/mo Free tier: Free credits to test

Cleanlist waterfalls a lookup across multiple data providers rather than relying on LinkedIn alone, at one flat rate regardless of data type — a direct answer to Kaspr's "LinkedIn-only, credits burn fast" complaint.

Tradeoff: it's a newer, narrower product than Kaspr's established Chrome-extension workflow, and doesn't have Kaspr's specific European phone-number reputation.

Where Kaspr still wins

Kaspr's Chrome extension genuinely is one of the fastest ways to reveal a phone number or email while browsing a LinkedIn profile, and its European phone coverage has a real reputation for accuracy. Its free plan is also one of the more generous in this category. If your prospecting is LinkedIn-first, mostly European, and you're comfortable tracking three credit types, Kaspr remains a reasonable, low-cost choice — which makes sense given it's positioned by its own parent company, Cognism, as the lighter entry point into that ecosystem.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best Kaspr alternative?

It depends on your team. LinkFinder AI suits teams that want one simple credit pool with no LinkedIn account requirements. Lusha suits teams that want a similar browser-extension workflow but with broader coverage beyond LinkedIn. Apollo.io suits teams that want a database plus outreach sequencing in one platform. Cognism (Kaspr's own parent company) suits enterprise teams needing global phone-verified data. Cleanlist suits teams that want multi-source enrichment beyond LinkedIn at a flat rate.

Why do people look for a Kaspr alternative?

The most common reasons are Kaspr's three separate credit pools (phone, direct email, and B2B email) that are easy to mismanage, a requirement of at least 20 connections on your personal LinkedIn account just to use the tool, and the fact that its "unlimited B2B emails" are generic addresses like info@ or contact@ rather than a specific person's direct email.

Does Kaspr require Sales Navigator?

No, but it's compatible with it. Kaspr's Chrome extension works on standard LinkedIn profiles, and also on Sales Navigator and Recruiter Lite for users who have those separate paid LinkedIn subscriptions — it isn't a strict requirement, just an available enhancement on top of Kaspr's own plans.

How much does Kaspr cost in 2026?

Kaspr's Starter plan runs about $49/month (or €45/month) billed annually, and Business is about $79/month annually, with a free plan offering roughly 5 phone credits, 5 direct email credits, and limited B2B email/export credits per month. Kaspr is owned by Cognism, which acquired it in April 2022.

Is LinkFinder AI GDPR compliant?

Yes. LinkFinder AI is based and operated in France within the EU and built to GDPR requirements from the ground up.
