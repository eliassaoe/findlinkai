1 LinkFinder AI Best for B2B contact & company data, with zero account-automation risk
Starting price: $49/mo (5,000 credits) Free tier: 150 credits, no card GDPR: Yes (France-based)

LinkFinder AI covers the data side of what most people use PhantomBuster for — email finder, LinkedIn profile enrichment, company data, and phone lookup — through a REST API rather than by automating actions from a logged-in LinkedIn session. There's no execution-hour quota, no phantom slot to configure, and no risk to your LinkedIn account, because nothing runs inside it. Why it's here: if your PhantomBuster use case is really "get me this person's email or enrich this company," this replaces that cleanly. 2 Apollo.io Best for prospecting, enrichment, and outreach sequencing in one tool
Starting price: Free, then ~$49–$119/user/mo (annual) Free tier: Yes, with limited credits

Apollo pairs a large contact database (275M+ contacts) with built-in email sequencing and CRM integration, so you're not stitching a scraping tool to a separate outreach tool the way PhantomBuster workflows often require.

Tradeoff: pricing is per-seat rather than usage-based, and Apollo doesn't automate LinkedIn actions the way PhantomBuster does — it's a database-and-sequencing tool, not a browser-automation one, so it solves a different part of the problem.

3 Waalaxy Best budget option for LinkedIn-only automation
Starting price: Free, then ~€19/mo (Pro) Free tier: Yes, 80 invitations/month

Waalaxy runs as a Chrome extension focused specifically on LinkedIn connection requests, messaging sequences, and profile visits, with pre-built campaign templates that are simpler to set up than chaining PhantomBuster's individual Phantoms.

Tradeoff: being a browser extension means sequences pause when your browser or computer is closed — no background execution the way PhantomBuster's cloud-based Phantoms run. Email capability is also locked behind its pricier Business tier.

4 Expandi Best for teams prioritizing LinkedIn account safety
Starting price: ~€99/mo per identity Free tier: Check their site

Expandi runs cloud-based with a dedicated IP per account, warm-up sequences, and configurable daily action limits designed specifically to reduce LinkedIn restriction risk — a direct answer to the account-safety complaints PhantomBuster users raise most often.

Tradeoff: it's priced per LinkedIn identity rather than per workspace, so costs scale directly with the number of accounts you automate, and it's a narrower tool than PhantomBuster — LinkedIn and email automation, not the broader cross-platform Phantom library.

5 La Growth Machine Best for true multichannel outreach (LinkedIn + email + Twitter)
Starting price: ~$60/mo per identity Free tier: Check their site

Unlike PhantomBuster, which is stronger at extraction than at multichannel outreach, La Growth Machine natively combines LinkedIn, email, and Twitter (X) into single sequences with a unified inbox — useful if your team's real bottleneck is coordinating replies across channels, not just scraping data.

Tradeoff: also priced per identity, and its core strength is sequencing and inbox management rather than PhantomBuster's breadth of pre-built Phantoms for miscellaneous scraping tasks outside LinkedIn.

6 Clay Best for advanced, multi-source enrichment workflows
Starting price: $185/mo (Launch) Free tier: 100 credits/month

Clay waterfalls enrichment requests across 150+ data providers and can chain in AI research steps per row, which goes further than PhantomBuster's built-in enrichment for teams building genuinely custom lead-scoring or research workflows.

Tradeoff: it doesn't automate LinkedIn outreach actions at all — it's a data and workflow tool, not a browser-automation one — and its own pricing and credit system (credits charged even on failed lookups) has drawn similar complaints to PhantomBuster's.

Where PhantomBuster still wins

PhantomBuster remains the strongest choice if you need one platform that spans LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Instagram, and general web scraping in a single workspace, with 100+ pre-built Phantoms covering use cases (post-commenter export, cross-platform data pulls) that none of the more focused tools above replicate individually.

Its workspace-based pricing is also genuinely fairer for teams running multiple LinkedIn accounts — a 5-person team on one PhantomBuster subscription can cost less than tools like Expandi that charge per identity.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best PhantomBuster alternative?

It depends on what you're using PhantomBuster for. If you mainly want B2B contact and company data — emails, LinkedIn profiles, phone numbers — LinkFinder AI covers that without the automation-related account risk. If you want continued LinkedIn outreach automation, Waalaxy and Expandi are the closest equivalents, with Expandi positioned as the safer, cloud-based option. Apollo.io and Clay serve teams that want a broader prospecting or enrichment platform instead.

Why do people look for a PhantomBuster alternative?

The most commonly cited reasons are LinkedIn account restriction or ban risk from automated actions, a pricing model split across three separate quotas (execution hours, phantom slots, and email/AI credits) that hit hard stops with no rollover, no permanent free plan (only a 14-day trial with heavily capped exports), and a learning curve when combining multiple Phantoms into a single workflow.

Can PhantomBuster get your LinkedIn account banned?

It's a documented risk rather than a hypothetical one — multiple reviewers report LinkedIn account warnings even while staying within PhantomBuster's recommended daily limits, since any tool automating actions from a personal LinkedIn session carries some exposure. Data-only tools like LinkFinder AI, which enrich records via API rather than automating actions from your logged-in account, don't carry this specific risk.

What's the cheapest PhantomBuster alternative?

For contact and company data specifically, LinkFinder AI's PAYG packs start at $25 and never expire, well under PhantomBuster's $69/month Starter plan. For LinkedIn outreach automation specifically, Waalaxy's entry plans start lower than PhantomBuster's, though full multichannel capability requires its higher tiers.

Is LinkFinder AI GDPR compliant?

Yes. LinkFinder AI is based and operated in France within the EU and built to GDPR requirements. PhantomBuster, also Paris-based, is GDPR compliant as well, so both are safe choices for EU-based teams on this specific point.

Do these PhantomBuster alternatives have an API?

Yes, most do. LinkFinder AI includes a full REST API on every plan starting at $49/month. PhantomBuster itself includes API access on all its paid plans too. Apollo.io and Clay are also API-accessible on their higher tiers.
