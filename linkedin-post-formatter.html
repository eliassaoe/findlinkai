How to use bold and italics on LinkedIn

LinkedIn's post editor has no formatting buttons for personal posts. The bold and italic text you see in other people's posts is made of Unicode characters that look like bold or italic letters. This formatter converts the words you select into those characters, so they keep their style when you paste the post into LinkedIn.

  1. Write or paste your post in the box above.
  2. Select a word or a line and click Bold, Italic, Underline or another style.
  3. Check the preview to see what shows before "see more".
  4. Click Copy formatted post and paste it into LinkedIn.

Preview the "see more" cut before you post

LinkedIn shows the first lines of a post in the feed and hides the rest behind "see more": about three lines, which is roughly 210 characters on desktop and fewer on mobile. Those first lines decide whether anyone clicks. The preview shows where your post is likely to fold on each, so you can put the hook above the cut. The exact cut depends on the reader's screen, so treat it as a close guide rather than an exact rule.

The counter tracks LinkedIn's 3,000-character limit for posts.

Use formatting sparingly

Bold a few words that carry the point, not whole paragraphs. Screen readers can read Unicode-styled letters oddly, and LinkedIn search does not match them as normal words, so keep keywords and names in plain text. Bullets and short paragraphs do more for readability than any style.

Frequently asked questions

How do I bold text in a LinkedIn post?

LinkedIn has no bold button for posts. Write your post in a formatter like this one, select the words, click Bold, then copy and paste the post into LinkedIn.

Does bold text work on LinkedIn mobile?

Yes. The styled characters are Unicode, so they display on desktop and mobile apps alike.

How many characters can a LinkedIn post have?

Up to 3,000 characters. The feed shows about the first three lines before "see more".

Is the formatter free?

Yes, with no sign-up. Your text stays in your browser.
