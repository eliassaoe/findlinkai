The 5 alternatives, ranked
Starting with the one we make, then four real alternatives for different needs.
1 LinkFinder AI Best for email, phone, and LinkedIn/company data in one tool
Starting price: $49/mo (5,000 credits) Free tier: 150 credits, no card GDPR: Yes (France-based)

LinkFinder AI covers email finder, phone lookup, LinkedIn profile enrichment, and company data through a dashboard or REST API, so you're not pairing Hunter's email-only tool with a separate phone-data provider for a multichannel sequence. Pricing is flat: $49, $89, or $149 a month for a fixed number of credits, plus PAYG packs from $25 that never expire.

Why it's here: if the lack of phone data is what's pushing you off Hunter.io, or you want one tool instead of two, this covers both in one place. It's not necessarily a fit if Hunter's specific domain-search workflow (bulk finding all emails at a company) is your main use case — see GetProspect or Apollo below for that.

2 Apollo.io Best for a bigger database plus outreach sequencing
Starting price: Free, then ~$49–$119/user/mo (annual) Free tier: Yes, with limited credits

Apollo's database (210M+ contacts, 30M+ companies) is roughly double Hunter's size, with 200+ search filters and built-in sequencing, a dialer, and CRM integration — replacing both Hunter and a separate outreach tool.

Tradeoff: pricing is per-seat rather than Hunter's flat per-plan model, and it's a bigger, more complex platform to learn than Hunter's single-purpose email finder.

3 GetProspect Best for unlimited team seats and more valid emails per plan
Starting price: Free, then paid tiers from $49/mo Free tier: Yes

At the same $49 entry price, independent side-by-side testing found GetProspect returns more valid emails than Hunter's Starter plan (1,000 valid emails and 2,000 verifications vs. Hunter's 500 emails and 1,000 verifications), plus a LinkedIn-specific extension Hunter doesn't offer.

Tradeoff: it's a narrower, less established brand than Hunter or Apollo, so it's worth testing against your specific target market before committing at volume.

4 Cognism Best for phone-verified data and intent signals at enterprise scale
Starting price: Custom, commonly ~$15,000–$25,000/year Free tier: None publicly listed

Cognism focuses solely on high-quality international B2B data — emails, phone numbers, and intent signals verified through a combination of AI and human review, including Do Not Call list checks in several European countries.

Tradeoff: it doesn't include email campaign or sequencing tools, and pricing is enterprise-style — custom quotes and annual contracts, a much bigger commitment than Hunter's self-serve plans.

5 Snov.io Best for email plus drip campaigns and a lightweight CRM bundled together
Starting price: Free, then ~$39/mo (Starter) Free tier: Yes, 50 credits/month

Snov.io pairs email finding and verification with drip campaigns, LinkedIn prospecting, and a built-in CRM — a genuine step beyond Hunter's find-and-verify-only scope, with unlimited team seats on every paid plan.

Tradeoff: LinkedIn automation is a separate $62-69/month add-on per account, and credits are shared across finding and verifying, so heavy usage burns through a plan faster than the sticker price suggests.

Where Hunter.io still wins

Hunter.io's pricing is genuinely transparent — full credit limits and feature breakdowns published on the pricing page, no demo call required — and every plan includes unlimited team members, which is rare in this category. Independent tests show verification accuracy under a 3% bounce rate, and the Chrome extension is simple and reliable for one-off lookups. If your only need is finding and verifying emails by domain, and you already have phone data and outreach tools elsewhere, Hunter.io remains a solid, no-nonsense choice.

Frequently asked questions
What is the best Hunter.io alternative?
It depends on your team. LinkFinder AI suits teams that want email, phone, and LinkedIn/company data in one flat-rate tool. Apollo.io suits teams that want a bigger database plus outreach sequencing. GetProspect suits teams that want unlimited team seats and more valid emails per plan. Cognism suits teams that need phone-verified data and intent signals at enterprise scale. Snov.io suits teams that want email plus drip campaigns and a lightweight CRM bundled together.
Why do people look for a Hunter.io alternative?
The most common reason is that Hunter.io only finds and verifies emails — it has no phone number data at all, which is a hard stop for teams running multichannel outreach with a calling step. Other reasons include a credit model that charges per email returned (not per successful search) and no built-in sequencing or CRM, so it typically needs to be paired with a separate outreach tool.
How much does Hunter.io cost in 2026?
Hunter.io offers a permanent free plan (50 credits/month), Starter at $49/month ($34/month annual, 2,000 credits, 3 email accounts), Growth at $149/month ($104/month annual, 10,000 credits), and Scale at $299/month ($209/month annual, 5,000 credits), with custom Enterprise pricing above that. Every plan includes unlimited team members, and email verification costs half a credit versus a full credit for finding an email.
Does Hunter.io have phone numbers?
No. Hunter.io is built specifically around email finding and verification and does not provide phone number data at all. Teams that need a calling step in their outreach typically pair Hunter.io with a separate phone-data tool, or choose an alternative that includes phone data natively.
Is LinkFinder AI GDPR compliant?
Yes. LinkFinder AI is based and operated in France within the EU and built to GDPR requirements from the ground up.
Does LinkFinder AI work with the same tools as Hunter.io?
Yes — LinkFinder AI integrates with n8n, Make, Zapier, and any platform that supports REST APIs, so it can slot into the same workflows Hunter.io users already have in place.
