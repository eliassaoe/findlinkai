How to extract data from a LinkedIn profile

  1. Copy the profile URL. Open the LinkedIn profile you want and copy its URL — e.g. linkedin.com/in/firstname-lastname.
  2. Paste it above and run the search. LinkFinder AI reads the public profile and structures it into clean fields — name, job title, company, location, industry, and more.
  3. Export or integrate. Copy individual fields, export a CSV for a whole list via Bulk Search, or pull results straight into your CRM through the API.

Why extracted data can go stale — and how we handle it

Static LinkedIn databases fall out of date the moment someone changes jobs, updates a title, or moves cities — some enrichment tools are working off records that are months old. LinkFinder AI pulls directly from the live public profile at the time of your search, so what you get reflects the profile as it exists today, not a cached snapshot from a database refresh cycle.
