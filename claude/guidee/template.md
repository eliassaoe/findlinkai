## Cover

> In this video we are going to see how to <...> using LinkFinder AI.

## Click script

A table. One row per recorded click. `Target` is the exact on-screen label
from `app-ui.md`. `Type` is what gets typed, verbatim, in one go.

| # | Action | Target | Type |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Step cards

The titles and narration that replace Guidde's auto-generated ones. Numbering
matches the click script.

**01 <Title>**
> <One sentence.>

## Closing card

The last step. Names the next action.

## YouTube

**Title:** <under 60 chars, keyword first>
**Description:** first line carries the target page URL, then a one-paragraph
summary, then timestamps, then the other videos in the series.

## Embed snippet

The `<iframe>` block to paste into the target page, matching how the existing
video pages already do it.
