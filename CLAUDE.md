# LinkFinder AI — YouTube channel production notes

Standing rules for anyone (including future sessions of this agent) producing
video content for the "Elias IA" / LinkFinder AI YouTube channel.

## Video length rules

- **Long-form videos must be AT LEAST 4 minutes (240s) of narrated content.**
  Do not ship a long-form video under 4 minutes. Target 4-5 minutes unless the
  user explicitly asks for a different length for a specific video.
- Shorts (vertical, 9:16) stay in the usual 15-45s range unless told otherwise.
- **Check this rule before finalizing any long-form build** — verify final VO
  duration is >= 240s before delivering. If a script comes in short, add real
  substance (more tools/tiers/beats, deeper detail) rather than padding with
  silence or repetition.
