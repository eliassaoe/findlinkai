What it does, in one breath

LinkFinder AI turns a LinkedIn profile, a name or a company into contact and company data: work email, phone, full profile, company details, employee lists. One lookup at a time in the app, a CSV of thousands, Google Sheets, HubSpot, or the API.

  • For paying customers, a LinkedIn profile returns an email 87–89% of the time, and a phone number 83% of the time (our own data, Nov 2026).
  • Pay as you go in credits: no seat licences, so a whole team shares one allocation.
  • Works where the data already lives: CSV, Google Sheets add-on, HubSpot, REST API.

How a deal works

  1. You get a partner code from us (write to us below).
  2. You start the conversation your way. When the prospect is interested, send them to linkfinderai.com/talk-to-sales?ref=YOURCODE to book a call, or book it with us directly and give your code.
  3. We run the call and the contract with you on it if you want. You can also close it yourself.
  4. The customer pays the annual invoice.
  5. Annual: 30 days after the payment clears (our refund window), we pay your whole commission. Monthly: we pay 30% of each payment the following month, for the customer's first 12 months. By bank transfer or PayPal, with an invoice from you.

A lead is yours for 90 days after it books a call with your code. If the customer comes back within the year and upgrades, the upgrade counts toward your deal.

The rules

  • Existing customers and leads already talking to us are not new deals. We'll tell you straight away if a lead is already ours.
