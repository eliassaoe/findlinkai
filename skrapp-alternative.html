Best Skrapp.io Alternatives in 2026

Skrapp.io is an easy, well-liked Chrome extension for finding emails from LinkedIn profiles and domains, with credits that roll over — a genuine plus most competitors don't offer. It also has a real accuracy gap worth knowing: Skrapp advertises a 92% success rate, but an independent benchmark of 5,000 contacts found a 42.8% verified rate, with other reviewers reporting real-world accuracy in the 65-75% range. It's also email-only (no phone numbers, no buying signals), and its Chrome extension scrapes LinkedIn in a way that carries account-ban risk under LinkedIn's terms of service. 2 Apollo.io Best for a database plus outreach sequencing bundled together
Starting price: Free, then ~$49–$119/user/mo (annual) Free tier: Yes, with limited credits

Apollo is the most commonly cited Skrapp replacement — a 275M+ contact database with built-in sequencing, a dialer, and basic intent data, replacing the "export to a separate sending tool" step Skrapp requires.

Tradeoff: pricing is per-seat and credits don't roll over (unlike Skrapp's), and the Organization tier requires a 3-seat minimum — a 5-person team on Professional runs $395/month before sending a single email.

Tradeoff: it's a point solution for email specifically — no LinkedIn profile browsing, no phone numbers, no bulk company enrichment beyond email.

Where Skrapp.io still wins

Skrapp's Chrome extension is genuinely simple and smooth for one-off LinkedIn lookups, its credits roll over month to month (unlike most competitors, including Apollo), and it holds a solid 4.4-4.5/5 rating across 250+ G2 reviews. For very small teams with tight budgets and minimal enrichment needs beyond basic email finding, it remains a reasonable, low-cost entry point.
