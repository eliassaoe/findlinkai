2 Apollo.io Best for a database plus built-in outreach sequencing
Starting price: Free, then ~$49–$119/user/mo (annual) Free tier: Yes, with limited credits

Apollo pairs a large contact database (275M+ contacts) with built-in email sequencing, a dialer, and CRM integration — enriching LinkedIn, Gmail, and Salesforce data in one Chrome extension, going further than ContactOut's lookup-only scope.

Tradeoff: pricing is per-seat rather than usage-based, and third-party reviews put real-world email accuracy around 65–70%, worth spot-checking against your own contact list.

3 Saleshandy Best for a large database with multichannel outreach built in
Starting price: ~$29/mo (Lead Starter) Free tier: 7-day trial, 5 free lead credits

Saleshandy's database (852M+ contacts by its own claim) is meaningfully larger than ContactOut's, with credits only charged when a verified contact is actually found, and cold-email sequencing available in the same product.

Tradeoff: Lead Finder is a separate subscription from Saleshandy's email-sending product, so a fully built-out outbound stack costs more than the entry price alone suggests.

4 Lusha Best for cold-calling teams prioritizing phone accuracy
Starting price: Free, then ~$37/user/mo Free tier: Yes, ~70 credits/month

Lusha has a real reputation for phone-number accuracy specifically, plus GDPR, CCPA, and ISO 27701 compliance, backed by a well-known Chrome extension workflow similar to ContactOut's.

Tradeoff: phone-reveal costs more credits than an email lookup, and Lusha's own annual-plan credits reset with no rollover — a different but comparable "the fine print matters" issue to ContactOut's fair-use caps.

5 Prospeo Best if verified email accuracy at low cost is your main need
Starting price: ~$0.01/email Free tier: 75 free emails/month

Prospeo is narrowly focused on one job — finding and verifying professional email addresses at a claimed 98% accuracy, refreshed every 7 days — at a transparent per-credit cost with no fair-use asterisk to watch for.

Tradeoff: it doesn't attempt ContactOut's LinkedIn profile browsing or Chrome-extension workflow — it's a point solution for email, not a full replacement for LinkedIn-based prospecting.

Where ContactOut still wins

ContactOut's Chrome extension is genuinely fast and well-liked for finding personal Gmail addresses alongside work emails directly from a LinkedIn profile, and its ATS integrations (Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, Bullhorn) make it a real contender for recruiting teams specifically. If your workflow is LinkedIn-first, low-volume, and you're comfortable staying under the ~2,000/1,000 fair-use ceiling, it remains a reasonable, fast tool for that specific job.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best ContactOut alternative?

It depends on your team. LinkFinder AI suits teams that want honest, published limits with no fair-use asterisk. Apollo.io suits teams that want a database plus outreach sequencing. Saleshandy suits teams wanting a large database with multichannel outreach. Lusha suits cold-calling teams prioritizing phone accuracy. Prospeo suits teams whose main need is verified email accuracy at low cost.

Why do people look for a ContactOut alternative?

The most common reasons are that ContactOut's "unlimited" paid plans are capped by a fair-use policy (around 2,000 emails and 1,000 phone numbers per month) that isn't disclosed on the pricing page, export quotas (as low as 300/month) that bind before the lookup allowance does, no overage option once you hit a cap, and self-serve plans locked to a single user per company.

Is ContactOut's "unlimited" plan really unlimited?

No. ContactOut's paid plans advertise unlimited emails and phones, but a fair-use policy in the Terms of Use caps usage at roughly 2,000 email credits and 1,000 phone credits per account per month, a restriction that doesn't appear on the pricing page itself. There's no self-serve way to buy additional credits once you hit the cap.

How much does ContactOut cost in 2026?

Third-party pricing trackers report a Free plan (5 lookups/day), a Sales plan around $79-99/month for email only, an Email+Phone plan around $99-199/month, and a custom Team/API tier. ContactOut's own pricing page has rendered inconsistently for some visitors, so figures are best confirmed directly with their sales team.

Is LinkFinder AI GDPR compliant?
