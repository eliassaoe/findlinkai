What GetProspect is — and where it's strong

GetProspect (getprospect.com) is a LinkedIn-focused B2B email finder built around a Chrome extension and web app, backed by a claimed database of 200M+ business contacts and 26M+ companies. Its free plan gives 50 valid emails a month with no card required, and — a genuinely fair model — it only spends a credit when it returns a valid, deliverable email; failed lookups don't cost you anything.

Its Chrome extension is a real strength: it holds roughly a 4.8 out of 5 rating from over 1,200 reviews on the Chrome Web Store, which is a strong, independently checkable number in a category where extension quality varies wildly. For teams whose day-to-day workflow is browsing LinkedIn or Sales Navigator profile by profile and pulling an email on the spot, that's hard to beat.

The short version: GetProspect wins on its large contact database, its highly-rated LinkedIn Chrome extension, and its pay-only-for-valid-emails credit model. LinkFinder AI wins on breadth of data per lookup (email, phone, LinkedIn profile, and company details vs. mostly email), native HubSpot two-way sync instead of a Zapier bridge, and an API-first / no-code-first workflow instead of a browser-extension-first one.

GetProspect alternative FAQ

What is GetProspect?
GetProspect (getprospect.com) is a LinkedIn-focused B2B email finder built around a Chrome extension and web app, backed by a database of 200M+ business contacts and 26M+ companies. Its free plan gives 50 valid emails per month with no card required, and it only deducts a credit when it returns a verified, deliverable email.
How much does GetProspect cost in 2026?
GetProspect has a permanent free plan (50 valid emails/month). Starter is $49/month billed monthly, or about $34/month billed annually, for 1,000 valid emails plus 5 phone credits. Growth is $99/month billed monthly, or about $69/month billed annually, for 5,000 valid emails. Higher-volume plans scale further for larger teams. Is GetProspect's Chrome extension good?

Does GetProspect have an API?
