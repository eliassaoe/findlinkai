LinkFinder AI gives you company and contact data enrichment without Wiza's required Sales Navigator add-on, LinkedIn-only data source, or per-lookup overage fees.

No Sales Navigator Tax

Wiza is built to scrape LinkedIn Sales Navigator searches, so a separate Sales Navigator subscription — another $79-149/month — is required to use it properly. LinkFinder AI works on its own, no LinkedIn subscription needed.

Beyond LinkedIn-Only Data

Wiza only enriches contacts sourced from LinkedIn Sales Navigator searches — conference leads, inbound forms, and partner referrals need a separate tool. LinkFinder AI isn't limited to one source.

No Surprise Overage Charges

Wiza charges $0.15 per email and $0.35 per phone number the moment you exceed your monthly allotment, and unused monthly credits expire at the end of the month. LinkFinder AI keeps pricing predictable.
