Questions

How do the monthly credits work?

Your plan's credits land on your account the moment you pay. Every month after that, your balance is refilled up to your monthly amount. Unused lifetime credits don't pile up from month to month, and credits you buy separately are never touched.

What does "lifetime" mean?

For as long as LinkFinder AI runs, with no further payment. It's tied to your LinkFinder account and can't be transferred or resold.
