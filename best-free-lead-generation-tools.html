Apollo works well for mid-market companies willing to invest time in setup and optimization.

  • Chrome extension for LinkedIn prospecting and data enrichment
  • CRM integration with Salesforce, HubSpot, and Pipedrive
  • Intent data signals showing companies actively researching solutions

Free plan available, paid plans start at $79/month – Free plan includes 50 mobile credits and unlimited email credits but limits exports to 10 per month.

#3

Hunter.io

Simple Email Finding and Verification

Hunter.io specializes in finding and verifying professional email addresses using domain searches and email patterns. The tool is beloved by freelancers and small teams for its straightforward interface and reliable email verification.

The free plan provides 25 searches monthly with email verification included. Hunter excels at finding emails from company domains but struggles with personal emails and generic addresses. It's perfect for targeted outreach to specific companies but less effective for bulk lead generation across multiple organizations.

Key Features

  • Domain search finds all public email addresses associated with a company
  • Email finder locates individual emails based on name and company
  • Email verification checks deliverability and identifies catch-all addresses
  • Chrome extension for finding emails while browsing websites
  • Bulk tasks process CSV files with hundreds of email lookups
  • API access for integrating email finding into your applications

Free plan available, paid plans start at $49/month – Free tier includes 25 monthly searches and 50 verifications, sufficient for small-scale prospecting.

✓ Pros

  • Extremely simple and intuitive interface
  • High email verification accuracy (95%+)
  • Useful free plan for testing and small projects
  • Excellent Chrome extension for quick lookups

✗ Cons

  • Limited to 25 free searches monthly
  • No phone number or social profile data
  • Struggles with personal email addresses
  • No built-in outreach or CRM features

LinkedIn Sales Navigator

Official LinkedIn Lead Generation Tool

LinkedIn Sales Navigator is the official premium tool for prospecting and lead generation on LinkedIn's platform. It provides advanced search filters, lead recommendations, and InMail credits for direct outreach to prospects.

Unlike LinkFinder AI which uses its own network, Sales Navigator requires you to use your personal LinkedIn account and follows LinkedIn's strict daily limits. The tool excels at finding decision-makers within target accounts but provides limited contact information without third-party enrichment tools. It's worth considering for enterprise sales teams selling to Fortune 500 companies where LinkedIn InMail has high response rates.

Key Features

  • Advanced search filters including seniority level, years in role, and company growth
  • Lead recommendations powered by LinkedIn's algorithm based on your preferences
  • Account insights showing job changes, company news, and engagement signals
  • 50 InMail credits monthly for direct outreach to prospects outside your network
  • CRM sync with Salesforce for automatic contact and activity logging
  • TeamLink shows connections through colleagues to warm introductions

Starting at $99/month – Includes 50 InMail credits and advanced search, but provides limited contact data without additional tools. One month free trial available.

✓ Pros

  • Most accurate and up-to-date LinkedIn data
  • Powerful search filters not available elsewhere
  • InMail often gets higher response rates than email
  • Official LinkedIn tool with zero ban risk

✗ Cons

  • Expensive at $99/month for limited features
  • No email addresses or phone numbers included
  • Requires purchasing additional tools for contact data
  • InMail credits don't roll over month-to-month

Lusha

Chrome Extension for Quick Contact Lookups

Lusha is a browser extension that instantly provides contact information while you browse LinkedIn, company websites, or other platforms. The tool prioritizes speed and convenience, showing emails and phone numbers in a single click.

The free plan offers 5 credits monthly, which is quite limited for serious prospecting. Lusha shines for sales reps who need occasional contact data during research, not teams running high-volume outbound campaigns. Email accuracy is solid for North American contacts but weaker for European and Asian markets.

Key Features

  • Instant contact reveal shows emails and phone numbers with one click
  • LinkedIn integration displays contact data directly on LinkedIn profiles
  • Bulk enrichment processes CSV files to find missing contact information
  • Email verification validates deliverability before adding to your list
  • CRM integration pushes contacts directly to Salesforce, HubSpot, or Pipedrive
  • Job change alerts notifies you when prospects switch companies

Free plan available, paid plans start at $39/month – Free tier includes only 5 credits monthly, limiting usefulness for active prospecting.

✓ Pros

  • Lightning-fast contact lookup via browser extension
  • Clean interface with minimal learning curve
  • Excellent mobile phone number coverage in US
  • Seamless CRM integration and data push

✗ Cons

  • Only 5 free credits monthly (very limiting)
  • Weaker data accuracy outside North America
  • No outreach or email campaign features
  • Premium plans become expensive quickly

Kaspr's phone number database is particularly strong for European contacts, where many competitors struggle. Enterprise B2B Contact Database

ZoomInfo is the gold standard for B2B contact data, offering the most comprehensive database of verified contacts, direct dials, and company information. The platform targets mid-market and enterprise sales teams with budgets to match.

While there's no truly free plan, ZoomInfo offers trials for qualified prospects. The platform excels in data accuracy, with researchers constantly verifying and updating contact information. Email deliverability rates typically exceed 95%, and direct dial phone numbers are accurate 90%+ of the time. The downside is cost—most companies pay $15,000-$30,000 annually for team access.

Key Features

  • 150M+ professional profiles with verified emails and direct dial numbers
  • Intent data identifies companies actively researching your solution category
  • Technographic filters target companies using specific software or tech stacks
  • Organizational charts map reporting structures and decision-makers
  • Real-time alerts notify your team of job changes and funding announcements
  • Native integrations with every major CRM and sales engagement platform

Custom pricing (typically $15,000-$30,000/year) – Free trial available for qualified businesses, but requires commitment to demo and sales process.

✓ Pros

  • Industry-leading data accuracy and coverage
  • Powerful intent data for timing outreach
  • Comprehensive technographic filters
  • Exceptional customer support and training

✗ Cons

  • Extremely expensive for small businesses
  • No transparent pricing (requires sales call)

The tool works well for teams prioritizing email deliverability over database size, though the smaller contact pool limits usefulness for niche industries.

  • Chrome extension for LinkedIn and web prospecting
  • Email pattern intelligence finds likely email formats for companies
  • Intent data shows companies researching relevant topics

Free trial with 5 credits, paid plans start at $74/month – Essentials plan includes 170 credits monthly with real-time verification on all contacts.

✓ Pros

  • Real-time verification prevents bad data

  • Competitive pricing for data quality provided

✗ Cons

  • Limited coverage for niche industries
  • Only 5 free credits for trial (very limited)

Global B2B Data with Diamond Verified Numbers

The tool excels for companies running global outbound campaigns who need reliable international contact data. Compliance features make it popular with European businesses navigating GDPR requirements, though the annual contract commitment limits flexibility.

  • Revenue intelligence tracks account engagement and buying signals

Custom pricing (typically $12,000-$25,000/year) – Free trial available but requires demo and qualification process before access.
