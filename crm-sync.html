How it works

  1. Connect your HubSpot account

    One-time authorization above, takes about 30 seconds. LinkFinder only requests access to contacts and companies — nothing else in your HubSpot.

  2. Choose what to track

    Email, LinkedIn URL, phone number — pick any combination, and tell us which HubSpot property each one should fill.

  3. Every Monday, we scan for gaps

    Up to your configured limit of contacts missing any of the fields you're tracking are checked per run, so cost stays predictable and your CRM gets covered gradually over time.

  4. Found values are written straight back to HubSpot

    Only into empty fields. Anything already in HubSpot — whether you or a teammate entered it — is never touched or overwritten.

Costs credits only for what's actually found and filled in — same rate as a normal lookup. Nothing is charged for contacts that already have a field, or that we can't find one for.
