    Questions people ask first

    Why is there no price on the Enterprise plan?

    Because the two things that move it — how many credits, and which data types — vary by a factor of fifty between accounts. A phone number costs 50 credits and a company lookup costs 1. Listing one number would be wrong for almost everyone. Agreements start at $999/month.

    Can I just stay on a self-serve plan?

    Yes, and most people should. Starter, Professional and Scale run from $29/mo annually up to $149/mo and need no call at all — see the plans. Come here when you need a contract, an SLA, an allocation above 50,000 credits a month, or a PO your finance team can process.

    What does "per meeting held" mean?

    A meeting that happened. Someone from your target list showed up and spoke to you. No-shows are not billed, and nothing is charged up front. Five a month is the floor, so $750/month is the smallest that engagement gets.

    Whose credits does the done-for-you work run on?
