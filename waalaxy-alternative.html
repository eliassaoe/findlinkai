Frequently asked questions
What's the cheapest Waalaxy alternative?
Dripify's annual Basic plan (~$39/user/mo) is closest to Waalaxy's own entry price. If you're actually after contact and company data rather than automation, LinkFinder AI starts at $49/month flat with PAYG packs from $25 that never expire.
Is Waalaxy worth the price?
For simple LinkedIn connection and messaging campaigns run from a Chrome extension, Waalaxy is a reasonable, easy-to-learn choice. Two things send people looking elsewhere: prices have roughly doubled since mid-2024, and email outreach is locked behind the pricier Business tier.
How is LinkFinder AI different from Waalaxy?
Waalaxy automates LinkedIn actions — connection requests, messages, campaigns — from a logged-in browser session. LinkFinder AI doesn't automate anything on LinkedIn; it enriches data (profiles, emails, phone numbers, company info) through an API and dashboard. If you need the outreach automation itself, Expandi, HeyReach, or Dripify are closer matches than LinkFinder AI.
Can I use LinkFinder AI instead of Waalaxy for lead generation?
For the data-enrichment half of lead generation, yes — LinkFinder AI gives you cleaner, more accurate LinkedIn and contact data that you can feed into your preferred outreach tool or CRM. It won't send connection requests or messages on your behalf; pair it with a dedicated automation tool for that part.
Can I try LinkFinder AI before paying?
Yes. Create a free account and get 150 credits to run your first searches at no cost, no credit card required.
Does LinkFinder AI carry the same LinkedIn account-restriction risk as Waalaxy?
No. LinkedIn restriction risk comes from tools that automate actions — connection requests, profile visits, messages — from your logged-in session, which is what Waalaxy, Expandi, HeyReach, and Dripify all do to varying degrees. LinkFinder AI enriches data through an API rather than acting on your LinkedIn account, so this specific risk doesn't apply.
How did you pick these 7 Waalaxy alternatives?
We included tools that come up repeatedly as Waalaxy alternatives for LinkedIn outreach: safer cloud-based automation (Expandi), agency-focused multi-account tools (HeyReach), a like-for-like budget swap (Dripify), genuine multichannel sequencing (La Growth Machine), cross-platform automation (PhantomBuster), and a warm-signal-based approach (Valley), alongside LinkFinder AI for teams whose real need is data rather than automation. We publish real starting prices and known limitations for each, including ones that don't favor LinkFinder AI.
Isn't this list biased since LinkFinder AI made it?
We're upfront that LinkFinder AI is our product and it's ranked first. That said, every entry lists real pricing and honest cons, including for LinkFinder AI (no LinkedIn automation or outreach sequencing at all). If your need is genuinely LinkedIn outreach automation, Expandi, HeyReach, Dripify, or La Growth Machine are the tools to look at, not us.
Does LinkFinder AI have an API?
Yes, and it's included on every plan at no extra cost. See the API documentation for endpoints and examples.
