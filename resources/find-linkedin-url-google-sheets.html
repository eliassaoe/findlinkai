Discover LinkedIn URLs Using LinkFinder AI Integration

You can find anyone's LinkedIn URL from names in Google Sheets. This demo shows how LinkFinder AI integrates seamlessly to enrich your data with LinkedIn profiles.

Go to linkfinderai.com

1. Access Integrations Section

In LinkFinder AI, go to the integration section to start connecting your tools.

2. Install Google Sheets Add-On

Click "Install the add-on" to add the Google Sheets integration for LinkFinder AI.

3. Open Google Workspace Marketplace

Switch to the Google Workspace Marketplace to find the LinkedIn profile URL Finder add-on.

4. Open Add-On Details

Click here to view the add-on details and installation options.

5. Proceed with Installation

Click here to continue with the installation process of the add-on.

6. Confirm Add-On Installation

Click "Install" to add the LinkedIn profile URL Finder add-on to your Google Sheets.

7. Sign In to Google Account

Switch to the Google Accounts sign-in page to authorize the add-on.

8. Authorize Access

Click here to proceed with account authorization for the add-on.

9. Select Permissions

Click "Select all" to grant the necessary permissions for LinkFinder AI to operate.

10. Enable Add-On Access

Fill "on" to enable the add-on's access within your Google account.

11. Open Google Sheets

Switch to your Google Sheets document where you want to use LinkFinder AI.

12. Access Extensions Menu

Click "Extensions" in the Google Sheets menu to find installed add-ons.

13. Launch Enrich Column Feature

Click "Enrich a column" to start enriching your spreadsheet data with LinkedIn URLs.

14. Return to LinkFinder AI Website

Switch back to the LinkFinder AI website to retrieve your API key.

15. Open API & MCP Section

Click "API & MCP" to access your API credentials and settings.

16. Copy API Key

Click here to copy your API key for use in the Google Sheets add-on.

17. Return to Google Sheets

Switch back to your Google Sheets document to enter the API key.

18. Open Add-On Settings

Click here to open the LinkFinder AI add-on settings within Google Sheets.

19. Enter Full Name Column

Fill the full name column with the names you want to find LinkedIn URLs for.
