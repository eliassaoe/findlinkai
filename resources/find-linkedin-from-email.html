How to Find a LinkedIn Profile From an Email Address

Sometimes you have the inbox but not the person — an inbound lead, a form fill, an old contact list. Here's how to reverse-lookup the matching LinkedIn profile from just an email address, with a confidence score on every result.

An email address alone doesn't tell you who someone is, what they do, or where they work — the context that makes outreach actually personal lives on their LinkedIn profile. Going from email to LinkedIn is the reverse of the usual lookup, and it's inherently a probabilistic match rather than a guaranteed one.

LinkFinder AI runs this reverse lookup and, importantly, returns a confidence score with each result — so you know whether to trust a match before you act on it. This guide covers a single lookup, bulk processing, filtering by confidence, and the API.

Tips for trustworthy reverse lookups

Always check the confidence score

Not every email-to-LinkedIn match is certain — the confidence field tells you how much to trust each result before you act on it.

Filter bulk results to high confidence

For large lists, filtering down to high-confidence matches before outreach avoids contacting the wrong person.

Use a personal or work email, not a shared inbox

Reverse lookups work best on individual email addresses — shared inboxes like info@ or support@ won't resolve to a single profile.

Re-run old lists periodically

People change jobs and LinkedIn profiles get updated — a stale contact export benefits from a fresh reverse lookup.
