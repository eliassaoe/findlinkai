How to Scrape LinkedIn Data With Claude via MCP

Connect LinkFinder AI to Claude once, then scrape LinkedIn profiles, company pages, emails, and employee lists just by asking — no dashboard, no copy-pasting between tabs.

Full walkthrough: scraping LinkedIn with LinkFinder AI's MCP connector · Powered by guidde

The usual LinkedIn scraping workflow means switching tabs — open LinkFinder AI, paste a URL, copy the result, switch back to wherever you're actually working. MCP (Model Context Protocol) removes that switch entirely: connect LinkFinder AI to Claude once, and every lookup after that happens right inside the conversation.

This guide covers the one-time connector setup, then two kinds of requests you can make once it's live: scraping a single profile or company page, and chaining follow-up questions — emails, employee lists — without leaving the conversation.

  1. Connect LinkFinder AI to Claude via MCP

1

Connect LinkFinder AI to Claude via MCP

A one-time setup — after this, every LinkedIn lookup happens inside your Claude conversation.

1

Create a free LinkFinder AI account

Go to linkfinderai.com and start a free trial — no card required.

2

Copy your MCP URL

In your dashboard, open the "API & MCP" tab and copy the MCP URL — your API key is already included in it.

3

Open Claude's Connectors settings

In Claude, go to your profile, then Settings, then Connectors.

4

Add LinkFinder AI as a custom connector

Click "Add", choose "Add a custom connector", name it "LinkFinder AI", paste your MCP URL, and click "Add".

Keep your MCP URL private — it has your API key baked in. Treat it like a password.
2

Scrape a LinkedIn profile or company with a prompt

Once the connector is live, just describe what you want in plain English.

1

Scrape a person's LinkedIn profile

Try a prompt like "scrape Bill Gates' LinkedIn profile with LinkFinder AI MCP" — Claude calls the connector and returns the full profile data in the chat.

2

Scrape a company's LinkedIn page

Ask for a company instead — for example, "scrape Microsoft's LinkedIn company page" — and get back the same structured data in the conversation.

3

Go further: emails and employee lists in the same conversation

The connector isn't limited to profile scraping — chain follow-up requests without starting over.

1

Find an email from the same conversation

Follow up with something like "now find Bill Gates' email address" — Claude reuses the context and returns the verified email.

2

List employees by seniority at a company

Try "find 10 employees who are C-suite at Tesla" — Claude pulls a filtered employee list straight from the company's LinkedIn page.

Each tool call still uses credits, the same as calling the API directly — the connector changes how you make the request, not what it costs.

Tips for getting the most out of the connector

Be specific in your prompt

Name the exact person, company, or LinkedIn URL and what you want back — "scrape [name]'s LinkedIn profile" works better than a vague ask.

Chain follow-ups in one conversation

Claude keeps context, so you can go from a profile scrape to an email lookup to an employee list without repeating who or what company you mean.

Treat your MCP URL like a secret

It has your API key embedded in it — don't paste it into a shared doc or public repo. Reset your API key in your dashboard if it ever leaks.

Watch your credit usage

Every tool call costs credits just like the API. If you're chaining several lookups per conversation, keep an eye on your balance.
