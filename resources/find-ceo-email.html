How to Find a CEO's Email Address

Reaching decision-makers starts with reaching the right inbox. Here's how to pull a company's executive team by seniority level and get verified emails for the CEO, founders, or C-suite — without a Sales Navigator seat.

Selling into a company usually means reaching a CEO, founder, or another C-suite executive — not a generic info@ inbox. The challenge is that executive contact details are the most protected on the internet, buried behind gatekeepers and stale directories.

LinkFinder AI approaches this from a company's employee list: filter by seniority level (Founder, Owner, C-Suite, Director, Partner) at a given domain, then enrich the executives you find with verified emails and phone numbers. If you already have a specific executive's LinkedIn URL, you can skip straight to enriching that one profile.

Find executives by seniority level at a company

Start from a company domain and filter down to just the people who make buying decisions.

Choose your input and output types

Set the input to Company Domain and the output to Company Employees List to pull a filtered roster instead of a single lookup.

Select the seniority levels you want — Founder, Owner, C-Suite, Director, or Partner — to narrow the list to decision-makers.

Review the results

Each result includes the executive's name, title, and LinkedIn URL — click through to confirm the right person before enriching further.

Enrich a known executive's LinkedIn profile

If you already have the CEO or executive's LinkedIn URL — from the list above or elsewhere — enrich it directly for their email and phone.

1

Switch input to LinkedIn Profile URL

Set the input type to LinkedIn Profile URL and choose an output such as Email Address or Phone Number.

2

Paste the LinkedIn URL and enrich

Enter the executive's LinkedIn profile URL and click Enrich Data to retrieve their contact details.

3

View the enriched contact data

LinkFinder AI returns the verified email and any other requested fields tied to that profile.

3

Find executive contacts programmatically via API

For pulling a company's leadership team into your own CRM or outbound tooling.

1

Open the Company Enrichment API docs

Go to API Access, then View API Docs, and open the B2B Data Lookup section under Company Enrichment.

2

Call company_domain_to_employees with a seniority filter

POST a request with the domain and a seniority parameter to pull just the executives you need.

3

Chain into linkedin_profile_to_email

Feed each executive's LinkedIn URL from that response into linkedin_profile_to_email to get verified addresses at scale.

Full request and response shapes are documented on the API documentation page.

Tips for reaching the right executive

Filter by seniority, not job title

Titles vary a lot between companies — filtering by seniority level (C-Suite, Founder, Director) catches more of the right people than searching a single title string.

Pull the whole leadership team, not just the CEO

A CEO's inbox is the most guarded. Founders, co-founders, and VPs are often easier to reach and just as influential on a deal.

Verify with a LinkedIn URL when you have one

If you already know exactly who you're targeting, searching by their LinkedIn profile URL is faster and more accurate than a name-based search.

Re-run key accounts periodically

Executive turnover is high. Re-enrich your top target accounts every few months so outreach doesn't land on someone who's left.
