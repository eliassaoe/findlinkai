LinkedIn Company Scraping: Pull Data for Many Companies at Once

One company's LinkedIn page is a quick manual check. A hundred-account target list isn't. Here's how to scrape structured LinkedIn company data — industry, headcount, specialties — for a whole list at once, without touching LinkedIn's own interface.

Try the free tool
No LinkedIn login required Bulk CSV supported Free to start

Account-based go-to-market motions live and die on firmographic data — industry, size, specialties, recent updates — and LinkedIn's company pages are one of the most current sources of it. The problem shows up the moment your target list has more than a handful of accounts: opening each LinkedIn page by hand doesn't scale, and scraping through your own logged-in session risks the account you're using.

LinkFinder AI reads LinkedIn company profiles programmatically, and the same lookup that works for one company works for a full CSV of them. This guide covers running it across a whole account list, and how the API extends it into a continuous pipeline.

Scrape LinkedIn company data for a list of accounts

Take your target account list — company names or LinkedIn URLs — and enrich all of it in one pass.

1

Prepare a CSV of your target accounts

A column of company LinkedIn URLs works directly; a column of company names will first be resolved to LinkedIn URLs internally.

2

Upload the CSV and set the output to LinkedIn Profile Data

Choose the bulk upload option, select your file, and set the output type to LinkedIn Profile Data so each row returns structured firmographic data.

3

Process the list

Run the enrichment across every row — industry, headcount, and other public profile fields come back for each company found.

4

Export the results

Download the enriched account list as a CSV, ready to load into your CRM or account-scoring model.

2

Automate LinkedIn company scraping via API

For account lists that update continuously rather than as a one-off export.

1

Open the API documentation

Go to API Access, then View API Docs, and find the LinkedIn company enrichment endpoint under B2B Data Lookup.

2

Call the endpoint per account

POST each company's LinkedIn URL (or name) as input_data to get structured profile data back in the response.

3

Sync results into your CRM

Feed the response into your CRM or data warehouse on a schedule to keep account data current without a manual re-export.

Full request and response shapes are documented on the API documentation page.

Tips for scraping company data at scale

No LinkedIn login, no ban risk

Because the scrape runs through LinkFinder AI's own infrastructure, there's nothing on your LinkedIn account to flag or rate-limit.

LinkedIn URLs match more reliably than names

If you already have company LinkedIn URLs in your CRM, use them directly — it skips the name-resolution step and reduces mismatches.

Cache what you pull

Company profile data changes slowly. Store results and re-scrape on a schedule (monthly or quarterly) rather than on every single run.

Use the API for ongoing account lists

A CSV export works for a one-off list; the API is the better fit once you're refreshing account data continuously.
