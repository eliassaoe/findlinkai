FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How is this different from buying LinkFinder AI credits?

The product gives you the data and you do the rest. This is the whole thing done for you — list, inboxes, copy, sending, replies. Same engine, none of the work.

Do I need my own domains and inboxes?

No. We buy and warm separate sending domains so your main domain is never exposed. They stay yours if we stop working together.

How long before I see results?

Sending starts once warmup finishes, usually two to three weeks in. First replies typically come in the same week sending begins. Listicle and backlink placements take longer because a person on the other side has to say yes.

Can I switch targets later?

Yes, that's the point of running all three. Plenty of clients start with customers and add listicles or backlinks once the first system is stable.

What do you need from me to start?

Your website, a description of who you want to reach, and an inbox to forward replies to. Everything else is on us.
