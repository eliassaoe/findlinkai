2 Apollo.io Best for a database plus built-in outreach sequencing
Starting price: Free, then ~$49–$119/user/mo (annual) Free tier: Yes, with limited credits

Apollo is the most commonly cited Clearbit replacement — it combines a large contact database (275M+ contacts) with email sequencing and CRM integration, adding execution tools Clearbit never had as a pure enrichment product.

Tradeoff: pricing is per-seat rather than usage-based, and Clearbit's company-level enrichment (firmographics, technographics) is generally considered stronger than Apollo's — Apollo's edge is contact volume, not data depth.

3 ZoomInfo Best for phone-verified data at enterprise scale
Starting price: Custom, quote-based Free tier: None publicly listed

ZoomInfo pairs a large, human-verified contact database with intent signals and outreach tooling — a genuine step up in scale and phone-number accuracy from what Clearbit ever offered.

Tradeoff: it's priced and sold like enterprise software, with custom quotes commonly running into five figures annually.

4 Cognism Best for EMEA-focused teams needing GDPR-first compliance
Starting price: Custom, commonly ~$15,000–$25,000/year Free tier: None publicly listed

Cognism is built around explicit-consent, GDPR-first data collection with strong European coverage and phone-verified mobile numbers — a common pick for teams selling internationally who need a defensible compliance story.

Tradeoff: like ZoomInfo, it's an enterprise sale with custom pricing and annual contracts, generally more expensive than SMB-focused options.

5 Clay Best for orchestrating enrichment across many providers yourself
Starting price: $185/mo (Launch) Free tier: 100 credits/month

Clay isn't a contact database like Clearbit was — it's a workflow tool that waterfalls a single lookup across 150+ data providers, useful if you want maximum flexibility and are willing to build the workflow yourself.

Tradeoff: it requires more setup time than a plug-and-play enrichment API, and its own credit model (charged even on failed lookups) has drawn complaints similar to what pushed people off Clearbit's pricing.

What actually happened to Clearbit

HubSpot acquired Clearbit in late 2023 and rebranded it Breeze Intelligence. Standalone free tools (the free Platform, Clearbit Connect, TAM Calculator, weekly visitor report) were sunset in 2025, and the developer API was deprecated for new non-HubSpot customers. Using Clearbit's data today requires a paid HubSpot subscription (Starter tier or above) plus separate Breeze Intelligence credit packs starting around $45/month for 100 credits, which expire every 30 days with no rollover. If you're already deep in the HubSpot ecosystem, that's a reasonable native add-on. If you're not, it's simply not accessible the way it used to be.

Frequently asked questions
Is Clearbit still available as a standalone product?
No. HubSpot acquired Clearbit in late 2023 and rebranded it Breeze Intelligence. Standalone free tools sunset in 2025, the Logo API was sunset, and Breeze Intelligence now requires a paid HubSpot subscription plus separate credit packs. If you're not already on HubSpot, Clearbit isn't really an option anymore.
What is the best Clearbit alternative?
It depends on your needs. LinkFinder AI suits teams that want flat-rate LinkedIn and company data enrichment without a HubSpot dependency. Apollo.io suits teams that want a database plus outreach sequencing. ZoomInfo suits enterprise teams needing phone-verified data at scale. Cognism suits EMEA-focused teams needing GDPR-first compliance. Clay suits teams that want to orchestrate enrichment across many providers themselves.
How much does Clearbit (Breeze Intelligence) cost now?
Breeze Intelligence starts around $45/month for 100 credits on annual billing, but that's on top of a required HubSpot subscription, which itself starts around $15-20/month at minimum and can run into the thousands per month at Professional or Enterprise tiers. Credits expire every 30 days with no rollover.
How much does LinkFinder AI cost?
Plans start at $49/month for 5,000 credits, up to $149/month for 50,000 credits, with pay-as-you-go packs from $25 that never expire. New accounts get 150 free credits with no credit card required, and there's no dependency on any other platform.
Is migrating from Clearbit to LinkFinder AI difficult?
Both are REST APIs that accept JSON, so the integration pattern is familiar. You'll need to update endpoint URLs and adjust response field names in your code, since the schemas differ, but the overall request/response shape stays the same. Full details are in our API documentation.
