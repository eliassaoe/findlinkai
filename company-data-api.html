import requests

url = 'https://api.linkfinderai.com/company_domain_to_employees'
headers = {
    'Authorization': 'Bearer YOUR_API_KEY',
    'Content-Type': 'application/json'
}
data = {
    'domain': 'stripe.com',
    'limit': 50
}

response = requests.post(url, headers=headers, json=data)
company_data = response.json()
print(f"Found {len(company_data['employees'])} employees")

Frequently asked questions
What company data can I access through the API?
Our company data API provides employee counts, company websites, LinkedIn company profiles, industry classifications, company size, founding year, and headquarters location. You can also access employee lists and contact information for key personnel.
How accurate is the company data?
We maintain 95% data accuracy through real-time updates and multiple data source verification. Our system continuously validates and refreshes company information to ensure you receive the most current business data available.
What's the API rate limit for company data requests?
Standard plans include 1000 requests per minute with burst capacity up to 2000 requests. Enterprise customers can request higher rate limits based on their specific use case and volume requirements.
Can I get employee data for specific companies?
Yes, our company_domain_to_employees endpoint provides detailed employee information including names, roles, LinkedIn profiles, and contact details. This data is perfect for building org charts and identifying key decision makers.
How do you ensure data compliance and privacy?
We're SOC 2 compliant and follow GDPR guidelines for all company data processing. All data is sourced from publicly available information and we provide opt-out mechanisms for individuals and companies when required.
