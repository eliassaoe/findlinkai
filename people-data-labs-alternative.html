2 ZoomInfo Best for verified phone data plus a full GTM execution platform
Starting price: Custom, quote-based Free tier: None publicly listed

ZoomInfo pairs a large, human-verified contact database with intent signals, org charts, and outreach tooling — a meaningfully broader platform than PDL's raw data API, useful for teams that want execution built in, not just data.

Tradeoff: it's priced and sold like enterprise software, with custom quotes commonly reaching into five figures annually — a different weight class than PDL's published per-credit pricing.

3 Apollo.io Best for a database plus built-in outreach sequencing
Starting price: Free, then ~$49–$119/user/mo (annual) Free tier: Yes, with limited credits

Apollo combines a large contact database (275M+ contacts) with email sequencing and CRM integration in one product, plus a self-serve dashboard PDL doesn't offer — useful if you want prospecting and outreach together rather than a pure data API.

Tradeoff: pricing is per-seat rather than usage-based, and third-party reviews put real-world email accuracy around 65–70%, worth spot-checking against your own contact list.

4 Crustdata Best for real-time, webhook-driven signals PDL's monthly refresh can't provide
Starting price: Permanent free tier; paid tiers custom-quoted Free tier: Yes, ongoing (not time-limited)

Where PDL's datasets update monthly by default, Crustdata is built around live crawling and webhook alerts for job changes, promotions, and funding events — useful for AI agents or automations that need to react the moment something changes.

Tradeoff: Crustdata doesn't publish pricing beyond its free tier, has a thin public review footprint, and offers no self-serve UI — it's aimed at engineering teams, not GTM or ops staff.

5 Cognism Best for EMEA-focused teams needing phone-verified data
Starting price: Custom, commonly ~$15,000–$25,000/year Free tier: None publicly listed

Cognism's phone-verified mobile data and strength in European coverage are frequently cited advantages for teams selling into the EU and UK, with daily-refreshed data and buying-signal alerts PDL's dataset model doesn't attempt.

Tradeoff: it's sold like enterprise software — custom quotes, annual contracts, no self-serve signup — a heavier commitment than PDL's developer-first, sign-up-and-go model.

A note on Clearbit

Clearbit used to be a common name on lists like this one, but it's no longer a standalone product to evaluate. HubSpot acquired Clearbit in late 2023 and folded it into "Breeze Intelligence," which now requires a paid HubSpot subscription plus separate credit packs — there's no independent API or free tier left. If you're not already on HubSpot, it's not really an option anymore, which is why it's not featured as a normal alternative above.

Where People Data Labs still wins

PDL remains a strong choice for developers who specifically want broad, raw person-profile coverage (3B+ profiles) through a clean, well-documented REST API, with transparent published pricing and a genuine free tier to test on. If your team is engineering-led and doesn't need a UI, dashboard, or CRM integration, PDL's developer-first design is a real advantage over broader, pricier platforms.

Frequently asked questions
What is the best People Data Labs alternative?
It depends on your use case. LinkFinder AI suits teams that want ready-to-use contact and company data through a simple dashboard or API. ZoomInfo suits teams needing verified phone data plus a full GTM platform. Apollo.io suits teams that want a database and outreach sequencing together. Crustdata suits engineering teams needing real-time, webhook-driven signals PDL's monthly refresh can't provide. Cognism suits EMEA-focused teams needing phone-verified data.
Why do people look for a People Data Labs alternative?
The most common reasons are that PDL's datasets refresh monthly by default rather than in real time, it has no self-serve dashboard for non-technical teammates (API-only), no native CRM or Zapier integration, and per-credit costs (around $0.28/credit on the Pro plan) that can add up quickly since bulk search requests can consume up to 100 credits at once.
How much does People Data Labs cost in 2026?
People Data Labs offers a free tier (100 person/company lookups per month), a Pro plan around $98/month (roughly 350 person credits and 1,000 company lookups, about $0.28 per credit), and custom Enterprise pricing for higher volumes.
Does People Data Labs have a self-serve dashboard?
No. People Data Labs is API-only with no web dashboard for browsing or enriching contacts and no CSV-upload workflow. Non-technical teammates typically need to request enrichment through engineering. LinkFinder AI offers both a dashboard and an API on the same account.
How much does LinkFinder AI cost?
Plans start at $49/month for 5,000 credits, up to $149/month for 50,000 credits, with pay-as-you-go packs from $25 that never expire. New accounts get 150 free credits with no credit card required.
