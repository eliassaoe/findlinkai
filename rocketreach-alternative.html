2 Apollo.io Best for a database plus built-in outreach sequencing
Starting price: Free, then ~$49–$119/user/mo (annual) Free tier: Yes, with limited credits

Apollo is the most commonly cited RocketReach replacement — a 275M+ contact database plus built-in email sequencing, a dialer, and meeting scheduling, replacing both RocketReach and a separate outreach tool.

Tradeoff: per-seat pricing still scales with headcount, and it's a broader platform than RocketReach's simple lookup tool, which means more surface area to learn.

3 Cleanlist Best for flat pricing that doesn't scale per seat
Starting price: ~$79/mo flat Free tier: Free credits to test

One independent 2026 test running the same 1,000-lead list across 10 RocketReach alternatives found Cleanlist had the best overall match rate (91%) at flat pricing that doesn't multiply per user — a real advantage for teams of 5+ where RocketReach's per-seat cost adds up fast.

Tradeoff: it's a newer, narrower product than RocketReach, without the Chrome extension workflow some users are used to.

4 Hunter.io Best if you only need email finding and verification
Starting price: Free, then ~$49/mo (Starter) Free tier: Yes, 25 searches/month

Hunter does one thing well — domain-based email search with verification — at accuracy comparable to RocketReach (~90% on verified addresses), with simpler, more transparent pricing.

Tradeoff: no phone numbers, no LinkedIn profile scraping, no sequencing — it's a lighter tool for a narrower job than RocketReach covers.

5 Cognism Best for EMEA-focused teams needing phone-verified data
Starting price: Custom, commonly ~$15,000–$25,000/year Free tier: None publicly listed

Cognism's human-verified "Diamond" phone data tests at the highest phone match rate among RocketReach alternatives in independent comparisons, a real advantage for teams whose bottleneck is direct-dial coverage.

Tradeoff: it's sold like enterprise software with custom quotes and annual contracts — a much bigger commitment than RocketReach's self-serve plans.

Where RocketReach still wins

RocketReach's Chrome extension and LinkedIn-native search workflow are genuinely convenient for individual recruiters and sales reps doing occasional, one-off manual lookups — you don't need to build anything, just search and click. If your volume is genuinely low and you're not trying to automate the workflow, RocketReach's simplicity is a real strength none of the API-first or platform alternatives above fully replicate.

Frequently asked questions
What is the best RocketReach alternative?
It depends on your team. LinkFinder AI suits teams that want API-first, automatable enrichment instead of browser-based manual lookups. Apollo.io suits teams that want a database plus outreach sequencing. Cleanlist suits teams that want flat pricing that doesn't scale per seat. Hunter.io suits teams that only need email finding. Cognism suits EMEA-focused teams needing phone-verified data.
Why do people look for a RocketReach alternative?
The most common reasons are per-seat pricing that gets expensive for teams (up to $209-249/month per user on the Ultimate plan), phone numbers gated behind higher tiers, annual lookup allowances rather than monthly, and independent testing showing real-world email accuracy around 71% and phone accuracy around 68%.
How much does RocketReach cost in 2026?
Individual plans run roughly $39-49/month (Essentials, annual lookup allowance, email only), $69-119/month (Pro, adds phone numbers), and $209-249/month (Ultimate, higher limits and API access), with lower annualized rates if billed yearly. Team pricing scales per seat.
How accurate is RocketReach's data?
One independent 2026 test against 500 known-valid contacts found RocketReach returned an email for about 78% of contacts, with about 91% of those valid on verification — roughly 71% real-world accuracy. Phone number accuracy tested lower, around 68%.
Does LinkFinder AI work with the same tools as RocketReach?
Yes — LinkFinder AI integrates with n8n, Make, Zapier, and any platform that supports REST APIs, so it can slot into the same CRM and outreach workflows RocketReach users already have in place.
