Questions

Before you sign up

What happens to the leads I paste in the free test?

We look up their emails and show them to you. Nothing is sent to the people on your list, and you don't need an account to see the results.

What does a credit buy?

A verified email from a LinkedIn profile is 10 credits, a mobile number is 50, and a LinkedIn URL from a name is 1. You only spend credits on what you ask for.

Can I test it before paying?

Yes, twice. Drop a CSV at the top of this page and get emails and phones for 5 leads without an account. Then the free account comes with 150 credits, enough for about 15 more emails, so you can check the data on a real list before you pay.

What happens on the setup call?

It comes with annual Professional or Scale. We connect LinkFinder to your stack (Clay, n8n, HubSpot, Google Sheets or the API) and run the first list you're working on for a client together, so your team leaves with a process that works. You get the booking link right after checkout.

Can I pay monthly instead?

Yes. Every plan is also sold monthly at the full price ($49, $99 or $249), cancel anytime. Yearly is 40% cheaper and includes the setup call on Professional and Scale.

Can someone help me set it up?

Yes. Book 15 minutes and we'll walk you through a demo and connect it to your stack.
