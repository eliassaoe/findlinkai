Questions

Before you sign up

What happens to the leads I paste in the free test?

We look up their emails and show them to you. Nothing is sent to the people on your list, and you don't need an account to see the results.

What does a credit buy?

A verified email from a LinkedIn profile is 10 credits, a mobile number is 50, and a LinkedIn URL from a name is 1. You only spend credits on what you ask for.

Can I test it before paying?

Yes, twice. Try 5 leads at the top of this page without an account. Then the free account comes with 150 credits, enough for about 15 more emails, so you can check the data on a real list before you pay.

How do I use the AGENCY50 code?

Pick Professional or Scale, then enter AGENCY50 on the checkout page. It takes 50% off your first month. After that the plan renews at its normal monthly price.

Can someone help me set it up?

Yes. Book 15 minutes and we'll walk you through a demo and connect it to your stack.
