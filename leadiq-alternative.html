What LeadIQ is — and where it's genuinely strong

LeadIQ is a B2B sales prospecting platform built around a Chrome extension that sits on top of LinkedIn and Sales Navigator, letting reps capture verified work emails and direct-dial phone numbers with a click as they browse profiles. Its real differentiator against a lot of the field is depth of workflow integration: native connections into Salesforce (including a managed package), HubSpot, Outreach, and Salesloft let captured contacts and job-change "champion tracking" signals flow directly into the sales engagement platform a team already runs on, without a Zapier bridge in between. It also ships Scribe, an AI email-drafting assistant that works inside Outreach, Salesloft, and Gmail.

LeadIQ holds a solid 4.2-out-of-5 rating on G2 across roughly 1,100+ reviews, with users commonly praising the LinkedIn-workflow capture and time saved on manual data entry. Its Chrome extension has meaningful adoption — reported around 40,000 users — though its Chrome Web Store star rating is reported inconsistently across sources (roughly 3.5 to 3.9 out of 5, from a fairly small review count), a noticeably more modest score than some other LinkedIn-focused extensions. What's genuinely worth knowing before you commit: LeadIQ's Free plan is capped at 50 credits a month that reset (not roll over), phone lookups cost 10 credits versus 1 for email, and its paid Pro tier is priced per seat with numbers that vary widely depending on the source — we could not independently confirm one consistent published rate, and Enterprise is a custom quote across every source we checked.

The short version: LeadIQ wins on its Chrome/Sales Navigator capture workflow, direct-dial phone data, and native Salesforce/Outreach/Salesloft integrations built for SDR teams. LinkFinder AI wins on transparent flat pricing with no sales call, a pay-as-you-go option where credits never expire, and a lookup that starts from a name, email, company name, or LinkedIn URL rather than only a browsed LinkedIn profile.

One credit covers 1 verified work email; a mobile or direct-dial phone number costs 10 credits. That 10x multiplier is worth budgeting around if phone data is central to your workflow — LeadIQ's genuine strength here comes at a real credit cost.

One thing we couldn't pin down. LeadIQ's paid tier beyond Free — commonly called Pro — does not appear to have one single, consistently published price. Independent pricing-research and marketplace sites we cross-checked cite figures ranging from roughly $15-20/user/month (described as an entry or annual rate) up to $150-200+/user/month (described as list pricing depending on volume and contract length), and some describe a three-tier Starter/Pro/Enterprise structure while others describe just Free/Pro/Enterprise. We're not going to present any single one of those numbers as confirmed fact — treat this as a case where, unlike LinkFinder AI's published flat pricing, you'll likely need to check leadiq.com/pricing directly or talk to LeadIQ to get your real number.   • Chrome extension built directly for the LinkedIn/Sales Navigator prospecting workflow
  • Direct-dial and mobile phone number data as a genuine specialty
  • Native integrations with Salesforce, HubSpot, Outreach, and Salesloft — not just Zapier

Where it falls short

  • Paid Pro-tier pricing is not consistently published across sources — no single clear number to point to
  • Free plan's 50 credits reset monthly rather than rolling over, and there's no pay-as-you-go option
  • Phone lookups cost 10x an email lookup (10 credits vs. 1)

  • Doesn't send emails, dial, or manage outreach sequences itself — still requires a separate sequencer or dialer
  • Phone-number accuracy is a recurring complaint across public reviews

LeadIQ alternative FAQ

What is LeadIQ?
LeadIQ is a B2B sales prospecting platform built around a Chrome extension that captures contact data directly from LinkedIn and Sales Navigator, plus direct-dial phone number data and native integrations into Salesforce, HubSpot, Outreach, and Salesloft. It's aimed at SDR teams already living inside a sales engagement platform.
How much does LeadIQ cost in 2026?
Free is $0/month for 1 user and 50 credits that reset monthly (no rollover). LeadIQ's paid Pro tier is priced per seat, and independent sources cite meaningfully different numbers for it — roughly $15-20/user/month as an entry rate in some places, $150-200+/user/month in others. Enterprise is consistently reported as custom-quote-only. Confirm current numbers at leadiq.com/pricing.
Does LeadIQ have a free plan?
Yes — 50 credits per month for 1 user, no card required, including the Chrome extension. The credits reset monthly but don't roll over. LinkFinder AI's free tier gives new accounts 150 free credits, no card required, as a one-time starting balance.
What's a good alternative to LeadIQ?
LinkFinder AI, if you want transparent published pricing with no sales call, a pay-as-you-go option where credits never expire, and a lookup that starts from a name, email, company name, or LinkedIn URL. LeadIQ stays a strong choice for SDR teams that specifically need its Chrome extension workflow, direct-dial phone data, and native Salesforce/Outreach/Salesloft integrations.
Is LeadIQ's Chrome extension good?
It has meaningful adoption — reported around 40,000 users — but its Chrome Web Store star rating is reported inconsistently across sources (roughly 3.5 to 3.9 out of 5, from a fairly small review count), which is notably more modest than some competing LinkedIn-focused extensions. Check the Chrome Web Store listing directly for the current rating.
Does LeadIQ integrate with Salesforce, HubSpot, Outreach, and Salesloft?
Yes, natively — including a Salesforce managed package and job-change/champion-tracking signals surfaced inside Salesloft. LeadIQ itself doesn't send emails, dial, or manage sequences — you still need a separate sequencer or dialer to act on the data.
Does LinkFinder AI have as many native integrations as LeadIQ?
No — LeadIQ's native Salesforce, HubSpot, Outreach, and Salesloft integrations are a genuine strength for SEP-centric teams. LinkFinder AI offers a native two-way HubSpot sync plus n8n, Zapier, and Make automation and a REST API, which is broader for custom workflows but narrower if your team runs entirely inside Outreach or Salesloft.
Is LinkFinder AI cheaper than LeadIQ?
LinkFinder AI's Starter plan is $49/month for 60,000 credits, published with no sales call. LeadIQ's Free plan is $0 for 50 credits/month, but its paid Pro tier is priced per seat with figures that vary across sources, and Enterprise requires a custom quote. Whether LinkFinder AI is cheaper depends on your volume, team size, and whether you specifically need LeadIQ's direct-dial data and SEP integrations.
