What actually happened to Proxycurl
The short version.

In January 2025, LinkedIn (via Microsoft) sued Proxycurl's parent company, Nubela, alleging it operated hundreds of thousands of fake accounts to scrape LinkedIn profiles at scale. The case settled and Proxycurl wound down its service in mid-2025, notifying customers and scheduling their data for deletion. Nubela's own next product, NinjaPear, and a separate lookalike, EnrichLayer, both emerged afterward — see the full roundup above for how each compares to LinkFinder AI.

Frequently asked questions
Is Proxycurl still operating?
No. Proxycurl shut down in mid-2025 after LinkedIn filed a federal lawsuit in January 2025 alleging it operated large numbers of fake accounts to scrape profiles at scale. The case settled and the service was wound down, with customer data scheduled for deletion.
What's the closest replacement to Proxycurl's exact API?
EnrichLayer launched after Proxycurl's shutdown with a very similar endpoint structure and credit-based pricing, and is widely described as a Proxycurl-style successor (though this hasn't been officially confirmed). If you need the closest possible drop-in replacement, it's worth evaluating directly. LinkFinder AI takes a different approach — flat monthly pricing and a single REST endpoint — rather than mirroring Proxycurl's exact API shape.
Does LinkFinder AI cover the same data as Proxycurl did?
LinkFinder AI covers LinkedIn profile enrichment, company data, email finder, and phone lookup, with batch CSV processing and JSON/Excel/CSV export. It won't be a field-for-field match to Proxycurl's old schema, so plan to check your specific data needs against our API documentation before migrating.
How much does LinkFinder AI cost?
Plans start at $49/month for 5,000 credits, up to $149/month for 50,000 credits, with pay-as-you-go packs from $25 that never expire. New accounts get 150 free credits with no credit card required.
Is migrating from a Proxycurl-based integration difficult?
Both are REST APIs that accept JSON, so the integration pattern is familiar even though the exact endpoints differ. You'll need to update endpoint URLs and response field names in your code, but the overall request/response shape (send an identifier, get back structured JSON) stays the same. Full details are in our API documentation.
Is NinjaPear the official Proxycurl successor?
NinjaPear is built by Nubela, the same team that built Proxycurl, and Nubela officially points former Proxycurl users toward it. But NinjaPear pivoted to company-customer intelligence rather than individual profile lookup, so it only replaces a slice of what most developers used Proxycurl for. See the full NinjaPear comparison above.
