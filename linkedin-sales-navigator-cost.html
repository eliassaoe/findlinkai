What you get for the money

  • Advanced lead and account search filters (seniority, function, company headcount, growth, years in role, and more).
  • Saved searches with alerts, saved lead and account lists.
  • InMail credits to message people outside your network.
  • On Advanced and Advanced Plus: team features, and on Advanced Plus, CRM sync.

What no Sales Navigator plan includes

No plan gives you a work email or a phone number for your leads, and no plan lets you export a lead list as a CSV. That surprises a lot of first-time buyers: you pay for the search, then you still need another tool to contact anyone outside InMail.

Do you need Sales Navigator at all?

It depends on what you use it for. If your team lives in InMail and account alerts, a seat earns its price. If you mainly use it to build lists and then hunt for emails elsewhere, you are paying twice.

LinkFinder AI's Lead Search covers the list-building part without a seat: give it job titles, keywords, locations, seniority and company size, and it returns LinkedIn profiles with work emails, 15 credits per lead with an email. A Starter plan is $49 a month for 5,000 credits.

Frequently asked questions

How much is LinkedIn Sales Navigator per month?

Core is $119.99 a month billed monthly or $89.99 a month billed yearly. Advanced is $159.99 monthly or $149.99 a month yearly. Advanced Plus is quoted. Prices vary by country and before tax.

Is there a free trial of Sales Navigator?

LinkedIn regularly offers a one-month free trial of Sales Navigator Core to eligible accounts. Check the offer shown on your own account.

Does Sales Navigator include email addresses?

No. Sales Navigator does not show work emails or phone numbers for leads. You need an enrichment tool for that step.

What is a cheaper way to build B2B lead lists?

If you mainly use Sales Navigator for lists, a lead search tool that returns profiles and emails together can replace that part. LinkFinder AI's Lead Search costs 15 credits per lead with a work email.
