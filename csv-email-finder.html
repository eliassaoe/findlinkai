Which input gives the best match rate

What your CSV hasExpected accuracy
LinkedIn profile URLBest. The URL identifies one specific person, so there is nothing to guess.
Full name plus company domainGood. Ambiguity only appears for very common names at very large companies.
Full name plus company nameFair. The company has to be resolved to a domain first, which adds a failure point.
Full name onlyNot usable. There is no way to tell which of six hundred people you mean.

If you can add a LinkedIn URL column before uploading, do. It is the single biggest lever on your match rate.

Verified, not permuted

Generating likely addresses is trivial and mostly useless — you can do it yourself with the free email permutator. The work is confirming which one exists. Sending to unverified permutations is the fastest way to damage a sending domain, and it is what most cheap lists actually are underneath.
