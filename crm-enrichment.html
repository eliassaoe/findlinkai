How continuous enrichment works

Set once. Runs on its own from then on.

1

Connect

Authorise your CRM. Nothing is written until you approve the field mapping.

2

Map fields

Choose which LinkFinder fields land in which CRM properties. Existing values are never overwritten.

3

Pick a schedule

Daily, weekly or on new-record-created. Only records missing data are looked up.

4

Stays fresh

New contacts get enriched as they arrive. Stale ones get re-checked. You do nothing.

You only pay for records that get enriched. A contact that already has an email costs nothing — LinkFinder skips it. Credit cost depends on what's being found, from 1 credit for company data up to 50 for a direct dial.
