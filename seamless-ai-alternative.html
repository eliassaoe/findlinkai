What Seamless.AI is — and where it's genuinely strong

Seamless.AI is a B2B sales intelligence platform built around a real-time prospecting search engine — it looks up and verifies contact data at the moment you search, rather than serving only from a static cache — plus a LinkedIn Chrome extension that's one of the most consistently praised parts of the product. It markets access to a very large database: figures across its own marketing materials range from roughly 1.3 to 1.7 billion contacts and 121 to 150 million company profiles, depending on which page you look at. Either way, that's a scale most self-serve enrichment tools, LinkFinder AI included, don't try to match.

Seamless.AI also holds a strong 4.4-out-of-5 rating on G2 across more than 5,000 reviews, with users regularly praising the speed of the Chrome extension and the real-time search itself. That's real, earned credibility for day-to-day usability. What's worth knowing before you commit is the pricing structure: only the entry-level Free and Basic tiers have a public price, and both Pro and Enterprise require a sales conversation and custom quote.

The short version: Seamless.AI wins on raw database size and live, real-time search. LinkFinder AI wins on transparent flat pricing with no sales call, a pay-as-you-go option where credits never expire, and a simpler feature set that doesn't require navigating add-ons and tiers.

Seamless.AI pricing, tier by tier

Seamless.AI uses a credit-metered model across four tiers. Only Free and Basic have a public price — Pro and Enterprise require talking to sales:

One credit is 1 phone or email lookup. A commonly repeated detail worth double-checking before you budget: on the Pro tier, credits are reported to refresh daily but expire that same night rather than accumulating — a "use it or lose it" structure, unlike a monthly pool you can draw down over the full billing period.

Two things to weigh. First, only the entry tiers have a published price — Pro and Enterprise pricing depends on a sales conversation, so you won't know your real monthly cost until you've talked to Seamless.AI. Second, several independent review sites report that credits are deducted for a lookup attempt regardless of whether the result turns out to be a valid email or working phone number. We haven't been able to verify that specific claim against Seamless.AI's own documentation, so treat it as a commonly cited user complaint rather than confirmed policy — but it's worth asking about directly if accurate cost-per-contact matters to you.

LinkFinder AI keeps pricing public and flat: Starter is $49/month for 60,000 credits, Professional is $89/month for 240,000 credits, and Enterprise is $149/month for 600,000 credits — all listed on the pricing page, no sales call required. If your usage is spiky rather than steady, Pay-As-You-Go packs ($25 for 1,000 credits, $75 for 3,500 credits, $200 for 10,000 credits) are also available, and those credits never expire.

Seamless.AI alternative FAQ

What is Seamless.AI?
Seamless.AI is a B2B sales intelligence platform built around a real-time search engine that verifies contact data at lookup time, plus a LinkedIn Chrome extension. It markets access to a large database — figures across its own materials range from roughly 1.3 to 1.7 billion contacts and 121 to 150 million company profiles.
How much does Seamless.AI cost in 2026?
Free is $0 for 1 user with 50 lifetime credits (no renewal). Basic is commonly reported at $147/month billed annually for roughly 250 credits. Pro and Enterprise require a sales quote — Pro is often reported in a $79-150/user/month range depending on team size and typically needs a multi-seat minimum.
Does Seamless.AI have a free plan?
Yes — 50 lifetime credits for 1 user, no card required. Unlike a recurring free tier, those credits are a one-time allocation, not a monthly refill. LinkFinder AI's free tier gives new accounts 150 free credits, no card required.
What's a good alternative to Seamless.AI?
LinkFinder AI, if you want transparent published pricing with no sales call, a pay-as-you-go option where credits never expire, and a simpler flat feature set. Seamless.AI stays reasonable if you specifically need its very large real-time database and Chrome extension workflow, and you're comfortable with quote-based pricing on the higher tiers.
Is Seamless.AI's data accurate?
Seamless.AI holds a strong 4.4/5 rating on G2 across 5,000+ reviews, largely for real-time search and the Chrome extension. Independent reviews also commonly report bounced emails, wrong phone numbers, and stale job titles in practice, and Seamless.AI's Trustpilot rating is far lower than its G2 rating — results appear to vary by use case and region.
Are Seamless.AI credits consumed even on bad results?
Several independent review sites report this as a recurring complaint — that a credit is deducted for the lookup attempt regardless of whether the result is a valid email or working phone number. We couldn't verify this directly against Seamless.AI's own documentation, so treat it as a commonly cited user complaint rather than confirmed policy, and confirm current behavior with Seamless.AI directly.
Does LinkFinder AI have as much data as Seamless.AI?
No — Seamless.AI's real-time search against a billion-plus-contact database is a genuine strength at a different scale than LinkFinder AI's core B2B fields. LinkFinder AI's differentiator isn't database size; it's transparent flat pricing, a pay-as-you-go option, and a simpler feature set.
Is LinkFinder AI cheaper than Seamless.AI?
LinkFinder AI's Starter plan is $49/month for 60,000 credits, published with no sales call. Seamless.AI's Basic plan is commonly reported at $147/month billed annually for around 250 credits, and Pro/Enterprise require a custom quote. Which is cheaper for you depends on your volume and whether you specifically need Seamless.AI's larger database or Chrome extension.
Can I try LinkFinder AI before paying?
Yes. New accounts get 150 free credits, no credit card required, so you can test real lookups before committing to a plan.
