Where Coresignal still wins

Coresignal remains a strong, affordable choice for teams that specifically need large-scale, structured company/employee/job data for analytics, investment research, or AI training — its published $49–$1,500/month tiers are transparent, and its multi-source, cleaned datasets (JSON, CSV, Parquet) are genuinely useful raw material for a data pipeline.

If contact data was never part of your use case — you're building an analytics product, not a sales tool — Coresignal's focus and pricing transparency are real advantages over broader (and pricier) platforms like ZoomInfo.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best Coresignal alternative?

It depends on what you need. LinkFinder AI is the simplest option if you want ready-to-use B2B contact and company data — including phone numbers — without technical setup. People Data Labs suits developers who want broader raw person-profile coverage. ZoomInfo suits teams that want phone-verified data plus a full GTM execution platform. Crustdata suits engineering teams that specifically need real-time, webhook-driven signals. Prospeo suits teams whose main gap is verified email accuracy.

Why do people look for a Coresignal alternative?

The most common reasons are that Coresignal has no verified emails or phone numbers in its dataset by design — it only covers firmographic, employee, and job-posting data from public sources — plus a query syntax (Elasticsearch Query DSL) that requires technical comfort, and records for smaller companies or less-active profiles that can be several months old despite Coresignal's real-time marketing framing.

Does Coresignal provide phone numbers or verified emails?

No. Coresignal provides firmographic data, employee profiles, and job postings sourced from the public web, but not verified emails or phone numbers. Teams that need outbound-ready contact data typically pair Coresignal with a separate verification tool, or choose an alternative that includes contact data natively.

How much does Coresignal cost in 2026?

Coresignal's published API plans start at $49/month (Starter), scaling to $800/month (Pro) and $1,500/month (Premium), all credit-based with a 14-day free trial (200 Collect and 400 Search credits, no card required). Bulk datasets are sold separately starting around $1,000 under custom contract.

Is LinkFinder AI GDPR compliant?

Yes. LinkFinder AI is based and operated in France within the EU and built to GDPR requirements from the ground up.

Do these Coresignal alternatives require technical setup?

It varies. LinkFinder AI works through a simple dashboard or API with no query language to learn. Coresignal, People Data Labs, and Crustdata are developer-first with no self-serve UI for non-technical users. ZoomInfo and Prospeo offer more accessible interfaces alongside their APIs.
